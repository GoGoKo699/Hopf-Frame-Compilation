## Main unfinished route and exact missing operation

A restricted family of complete real frames already contains every prescribed
Boolean table. One such frame, its actual inverse and two CNOTs implement
the XOR of the table value into an arbitrary target with work return.
Consequently a lower bound for these prescribed queries can constrain
every general frame compiler at the corresponding allocation.
[Boolean query inside a frame](research/depth/BOOLEAN_QUERY_FRAME_REDUCTION.md)

In the constructive direction, [R98](research/depth/QUERY_DEPTH_FRAME_TRANSFER.md)
accepts a complete **word** query. A separate [scalar transfer](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md)
uses prescribed sign tables across widths with an iterated-logarithm cost.
A sufficiently sublogarithmic scalar source improves full-frame depth
at fixed precision. The proof charges its terminal, inverse and work.
The [word-query accuracy result](research/depth/APPROXIMATE_QUERY_FRAME_TRANSFER.md)
and the [scalar precision wrapper](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#pair-reflection-scalar-amplification)
have different source hypotheses. A fixed-error scalar source with exact
physical address preservation is one sufficient premise. More generally,
a [full-space signed gap](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#full-space-signed-gap-source)
permits nonflat spectra and completed address motion, with logarithmic
amplification cost and no additional clean qubits. A
[one-clean coherent decision](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#one-clean-decision-source)
weakens the source requirement further: amplify on its initialized code
before promoting it to complete return. The raw source may leave garbage,
and its rejected clean sector need not carry the table's prescribed sign.

**Resumption question.** Can a genuinely joint native word V turn a known
table f into a uniformly biased one-clean decision? Let J append
$`c=0`$, put $`M=J^\dagger V^\dagger Z_cVJ`$, and require
$`\mathrm{sgn}(M)=D_f\otimes I`$ and
$`|M|\succeq\gamma I`$ for a uniform $`\gamma\gt0`$.
Pay V and its actual reverse with one clean input, T-count and dirty
width $`O(2^{a/2})`$, and Clifford count $`O(2^a)`$. The sign
condition holds on the entire initialized code, including arbitrary dirty
and reference inputs; basis-state success probabilities alone do not imply it.
The [square-root-width transfer](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#square-root-width-signed-gap-transfer)
then needs only
$`(1+\log^*a)\log(3+\log^*a)d(a)=o(\log a)`$.
Even $`d(a)=O(\log a/\log\log a)`$ suffices for a first general
improvement at $`b=K_L\sqrt N`$. An all-width source theorem can wait.

The [two-source-band screen](research/depth/FOUR_LAYER_SCALAR_GAP.md#two-band-signed-gap-sources)
forces an exponentially small gap for a single marked table and its
Pauli-balanced lift, independent of width or address motion. A search
within the full-space Pauli-signal interface must therefore move beyond two raw
T bands and use nontrivial joint transport. The one-clean decision proof
also records why code compression does not evade that screen. The
[four-call shear reduction](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#self-inverse-shear-tables)
still makes scalar entry readout for known self-inverse CNOT shears a
sufficient concrete target. Amplify each correct entry decision or full-space gap
before applying that reduction; the four raw gapped signals need not
themselves compose into a gapped signal.

The [fixed-boundary information bound](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#fixed-boundary-decision-source)
excludes the stated read-only programs and outer-Clifford extension.
An [interior Clifford](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#interior-clifford-decision-source)
gives table-sized decoder data, but [paired-row singlets](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#paired-row-decision-obstruction)
defeat swap-preserving boundaries, including the graph phase and both table shears.
The [explicit symmetry breaker](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#explicit-symmetry-breaker-failure)
avoids those sectors yet fails on arbitrarily large nonconstant tables.
The [all-ones spectral obstruction](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#boundary-independent-spectral-obstruction)
excludes every conjugating boundary for this unchanged middle word as a universal literal-table prescription.
Separate constant-table handling or preprocessing can escape; asymmetric boundaries for other tables remain open.
Resume with a complete compressed-spectrum identity for a changed word or an explicit exception/preprocessing scheme.
Joint table/address interleaving and physical address motion inside the word remain constructive alternatives.

The parallel lower-bound question remains unrestricted returned-query depth with arbitrary
Clifford interlayers; the two-band argument does not iterate to additional native bands.
The [raw-decision count bound](research/depth/BOOLEAN_QUERY_FRAME_REDUCTION.md#raw-decision-count)
is now tight for strict computational-basis bias: a [paid controlled Gauss word](research/depth/GAUSS_SUM_QUERY_ENCODING.md#constant-depth-raw-decisions)
represents every table with square-root T-count/width and constant native depth.
Every computational dirty input has the correct exponentially small bias, yet two fixed
product superpositions give opposite deterministic answers at every address. Counting these
strict-sign patterns cannot prove growing depth; a new invariant must use a quantitative bias
requirement, coherent sign or complete return. The deliverable remains a qualifying source or such an invariant.
An isolated marked address cannot force growing fixed-error depth:
[expander zero reflections](research/depth/EXPANDER_ZERO_REFLECTION.md) already have constant depth.

The marked feedback-edge pencil remains an alternative encoding with a paid
constant-depth matrix–vector XOR. Its [thin invariant-projector readout](research/depth/PRESCRIBED_SCALAR_TABLES.md#thin-kernel-rank-obstruction)
cannot produce the full scalar sign with bounded clean work and polynomially
many insertions when the erased word fixes the code and the address sectors
are preserved. Reopen that extraction by changing its signal support or
baseline, rather than merely conjugating the same small projector. A lower
bound for the general problem can instead use the deepest-layer family
above and must allow arbitrary Clifford interlayers, table-specific
compilation and the allowed dirty width.

### Other retained results and alternatives

At $`b=\Theta(\sqrt N)`$, [prefix-shared rotations](research/depth/PREFIX_ROTATION_PROFILES.md)
give sublogarithmic depth on complete real frames with $`q=n^{o(1)}`$
arbitrary top levels and one rotation per lower depth within each resulting
subtree. Independent deepest-layer entries still include every Boolean table.
The [four-layer scalar gap](research/depth/FOUR_LAYER_SCALAR_GAP.md)
closes the zero-clean exactly silent single-mark route at seven or more
address bits. It concerns a different allocation from the general one-clean gap.

The augmented field recoupler has an exact logarithmic-depth construction
using the established dirty indicator. Its gate and width ledgers fit the
relaxed discovery regime. Replacing that indicator with the resulting
decoder supplies no sublogarithmic recurrence.
[Logarithmic augmented field recoupler](research/depth/AUGMENTED_FIELD_RECOUPLER.md#7-a-logarithmic-depth-decoder-for-the-full-augmented-query)

The complete indicator remains a sufficient alternative. For a p-bit
address and $`m=2^p`$, it must toggle the selected bit of an arbitrary
bank and return every helper. At each fixed full-operator tolerance,
the target has zero clean inputs, $`O(m)`$ T gates and dirty qubits,
$`O(m^2)`$ elementary Cliffords and $`o(\log(p+2))`$ native T-depth. Reopen a field-decoder, mask or fixed-layer route when
it supplies a mechanism for changing this scaling or a lower bound on the
actual frame model. [Indicator fusion](research/depth/POLYNOMIAL_OVERHEAD_INDICATOR_FUSION.md) ·
[Field decisions](research/ROUTE_DECISIONS.md#field-recoupling)
