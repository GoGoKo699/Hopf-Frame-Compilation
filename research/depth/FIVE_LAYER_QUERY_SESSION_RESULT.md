## 1. What changed

The [five-layer proof home](READ_ONLY_FIVE_LAYER_REDUCTION.md) replaces
the failed repeated-peeling argument with one outer-layer reduction.
The comparison family K remains Clifford, but its cocycle is conjugated
by a two-T-dressed Pauli representation B. At exact return, B normalizes
the particular Clifford subgroup generated by K. It need not normalize
the physical Pauli group. Thus the missing step is now a specific
partial-normalizer invariant, rather than an assumed closure of the
Clifford hierarchy.

One subcase closes: **if the specified B family is Clifford-valued**, a
five-layer XOR query has Boolean degree at most seven at full operator
error below $`\sqrt2/12`$, independent of dirty width. When the target
changes the outer symplectic shear, the bound improves to four. The
proof uses a translation-invariant space of Boolean functions in that
case, and explicit Hermitian-Pauli conjugation signs in the other case.
Neither bound is asserted sharp. The extra hypothesis is indispensable
to the present proof and is not inferred from workspace return.

A literal native affine-query fixture has non-Clifford B and full return.
It simplifies to a single CNOT. Its purpose is to falsify the proposed
inference about every displayed representation, not to improve a gate
count. A related one-qubit example also shows that a non-Clifford B can
normalize an irreducible subgroup of Cliffords. These examples do not
construct a high-degree five-layer query.

## 2. Constructive tests and their diagnoses

The [phase-relocation construction](READ_ONLY_PHASE_RELOCATION.md)
uses an exact null phase identity to move an affine-parity T phase
onto four arbitrary dirty qubits in four physical T layers. It charges
15 T gates and $`22+16h+64\kappa`$ elementary Cliffords for a parity
with h variable terms and constant bit kappa. Every computation is
actually reversed; phases and work return are exact for every input.

Seven private copies give a scalar CCZ on three untouched address bits:
105 T gates, 346 elementary Cliffords, four T layers, zero clean qubits
and 28 simultaneous dirty helpers, with zero error and leakage. Together
with the retained three-layer obstruction, this makes the scalar
degree-three boundary at four layers sharp in the read-only interface.
The existing four-layer AND remains much cheaper. Applying relocation
to R91's bilinear phase batch reduces that component's layer constant
from eight to four while increasing count and width constants; it does
not reduce the address-indicator cost or improve the general upper law.

This construction superseded a five-layer packing of a different parity
list. The abandoned rank argument was representation-dependent: adding
null phase identities changes both the list and its work-parity rank.
A lower-bound parameter must survive such changes. Purely diagonal
parity synthesis still has weighted degree at most three and cannot
produce the sought cubic XOR query by a target Hadamard conversion.

A second escape put arbitrarily high degree into a fixed Boolean
diagonal source and tried to extract it by one returned translation
echo. The [echo theorem](RETURNED_DIAGONAL_ECHO_DEGREE.md) proves

```math
\Delta_{Lx}\Delta_{Mx}h(z)=q(x)\quad\text{for every }z
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \deg q\le3.
```

The bound is independent of source degree, dirty width, and dependencies
or overlaps among the linear translation directions. Cubic degree is
attained by a fully charged native example. This is an exact theorem
for that echo, with no approximation extension or arbitrary-conjugator
claim. Hiding more complexity in the source alone cannot improve it.

## 3. What remains established and open

The count package remains settled under its stated hypotheses. For
$`n\ge1,L\ge6`$, R96 remains the strongest unconditional general law:

```math
T=O(\sqrt{NL}+L+NL/b),\qquad G=O(NL),\qquad
D_T=O(NL/b^2+L+\log_2(n+2)).
```

It uses one clean qubit, full initialized-isometry error $`2^{-L}`$,
and literal dirty thresholds $`17(L+n+7)`$ for real frames or
$`17(L+n+8)`$ for complex magnitude frames. Every logical column,
literal sign and phase, singular and zero coefficient, dirty/reference
input, source, query, control, recovery, actual inverse, leakage and
workspace return retains its charge. R79 remains selected Result D.
R97 retains its outer-misalignment hypotheses; unrestricted one-clean
four-layer rigidity remains open. R98 remains conditional on its exact
multi-output query and simultaneous resource envelope.

The general zero-clean five-layer read-only degree question also remains
open: this round proves a conditional subcase, not the general case.
There is no new dimension-growing unrestricted frame lower bound and
no improved asymptotic complete-frame upper bound. Final manuscript
drafting is unchanged.

## 4. Concrete resumption question

The most useful next question is whether conjugation by a native
two-T-dressed Pauli has a **bounded-degree action on binary Clifford
tableaus on the subset it maps back into Cliffords**, with the degree
independent of physical width. The five-layer cocycle imposes additional
consistency across addresses; it must be retained if a general
partial-normalizer assertion fails. Complex linearity of unitary
conjugation does not itself give the required binary-polynomial action.

Success would extend the closed Clifford-middle subcase and could give
the next step toward a uniform degree-versus-depth law. A counterexample
would identify the non-diagonal mechanism a constructive query should
exploit. The phase identity and full-return echo theorem are useful tests
of either proposal: fixed parity-list rank and source degree alone are
not adequate invariants.

The broader question remains whether exact zero-clean read-only queries
satisfy $`\deg f\le2^{Cd}`$ for an absolute C at d physical T layers,
or admit unbounded degree at a fixed d; intermediate growth is possible.
An exponential bound would require logarithmic depth for address AND
within this interface. A constructive escape must additionally preserve
R91's T-count, Clifford count and simultaneous allocation before R98
improves the complete circuit. Fixed-error robustness at growing depth
and unrestricted address mixing remain separate requirements for a
general frame lower bound.

## 5. Evidence and pause

The new proof homes link bounded checks of actual native words, inverse
identities, literal phases, workspace return, resource ledgers, the
normalizer counterexample, and the Boolean-algebra steps. The universal
statements are analytic; finite checks do not extrapolate in width.
Independent reviews checked the phase relocation, returned echo, and
conditional five-layer argument. Validation and presentation results
are recorded with the merge on the reviewed tree.

The direct repeated-peeling and fixed-list rank candidates were stopped
after their failures were identified. The normalizer reformulation and
the precise question above preserve the general objective for resumption.
