# Arbitrary angular defects with bounded nesting in a complete Haar frame

In the positive balanced real n-qubit frame, replace the rotations at s tree nodes by independently prescribed real rotations. Allow defects at any depths. Suppose a root-to-leaf branch meets at most K defects, with K fixed. At fixed full-frame precision ε=2^{-L}, square-root dirty width, and log log(s+2)=o(log(n+2)), there is an unconditional one-clean native compiler with
```math
 D_T=O_{K,L}(\log\log(n+4)+1+\log\log(s+2)),\qquad
 T=O_{K,L}(\sqrt{2^n}),\qquad G=O_{K,L}(2^n).
```
Every supplied work state, entangled reference and frame column is allowed. In particular polynomially many defects have doubly logarithmic depth even if their locations use every possible scale allowed by bounded nesting. The simultaneous resource conclusion also holds when s=2^{o(n)} and the displayed depth need not improve on the unrestricted upper there. The depth theorem uses [complete positive Haar and its three-basis dirty vote](POSITIVE_HAAR_FINGERPRINT.md). Either its elementary zero-test consequence proved below or the [constant-depth read-only equality](GATEWISE_READ_ONLY_EQUALITY.md) can supply the wide guard. All components besides the final positive Haar use poly_{K,L}(n,s+2) counts and dirty width. In the indicated asymptotic regimes these fit by a dimension cutoff; for finitely many smaller n use R96. The argument gives no improvement if the maximum defect nesting grows arbitrarily.

## The input plane of a changed rotation

Index input basis vectors by the global root marker 0^n and one mother marker b_v for each internal binary prefix v, represented by v 1 0^{n−|v|−1}. Define a_empty as the root marker. Recursively let
```math
 a_{v0}=(a_v-b_v)/\sqrt2,\qquad a_{v1}=(a_v+b_v)/\sqrt2 .
```
This follows by taking the adjoint of the balanced rotation R(π/4) acting on the old father and the newly born mother, using the column convention R(θ)=[[cosθ,−sinθ],[sinθ,cosθ]]. The father a_v is the inverse positive prefix transform of the node basis state. For a perturbation of angle δ_v, the relative input-basis factor is exactly the real rotation by δ_v in the oriented plane (a_v,b_v), identity on its orthogonal complement. This follows by conjugating the changed elementary layer by the product of the preceding ideal layers. The full perturbed frame is the positive full frame times these relative factors in decreasing depth from left to right. This factorization remains exact when many rotations change.

For incomparable v,v', the two planes are orthogonal: under the full ideal Haar they map to vectors supported on disjoint leaf-prefix cylinders. Hence their relative factors commute. Sort nodes into antichains by their number of strictly marked ancestors. The sorted product, in descending ancestor count from left to right, agrees with the original because the relative order of every comparable pair is unchanged and incomparable pairs commute. There are at most K antichains.

## Local Haar trees in two passes

It suffices to implement one antichain. Choose the integer l=max(1,ceil(2 log₂(C(s+1)/ε))) with a sufficiently large absolute constant, increasing C for a fixed error allocation. For each node v define g=floor(|v|/l), c=max(0,(g−1)l), and attach v to the patch indexed by g and its prefix p of length c. A patch has a virtual complete binary tree of height m=2l. If c=0, choose its root to be the actual global root marker. Otherwise allocate a distinct computational basis label for its dummy root, avoiding ALL genuine marker labels appearing in any true father or changed mother in the entire task. There are at most 1+s(n+1) such labels plus s dummies; the dimensional cutoff in the sparse regime permits this. Using just the simple injective hash below also requires room for O(log(s+2)+log l) data bits plus temporary clean bits; this holds at large n when s=2^{o(n)}. Reuse of the genuine root across passes is allowed.

The local father at v is constructed by the same signed Haar recurrence from the root of its patch, using actual mother markers for nodes from p down to the strict parent of v. It equals the true father for c=0. If c>0, their difference is exactly 2^{−(|v|−c)/2} times the difference of the local root and the original a_p. Thus the vector-norm discrepancy is at most 2·2^{−l/2}. The rotating mother is identical. For any two unit fathers a,a' orthogonal to b, the difference of the two plane rotations in operator norm is at most 6||a−a'|| by expanding
```math
 I+(\cos\delta-1)(|a\rangle\langle a|+|b\rangle\langle b|)
       +\sin\delta(|b\rangle\langle a|-|a\rangle\langle b|).
```
The comparison of the product costs at most 12 s 2^{-l/2} over the entire task. This price includes all coherent superpositions.

Divide groups g into even and odd passes. Within each pass the real mother markers needed by different patches are disjoint. For equal g their prefixes p of length c differ. For groups spaced by at least two, the range of real nodes in a patch starts at depth c and ends before (g+1)l, whereas that of g+2 starts at (g+1)l. The real root marker occurs in at most one patch per pass, and all dummy roots are fresh. Thus each pass is a direct sum of disjoint patch actions after approximate replacement. This grouping is legitimate because the original factors in the antichain commute; compare the replacement product to that chosen order, and never assume commuting factors from different antichains.

## Giving the local circuits room to work

Let U be the union of all real mothers and nearby ancestors used by the local fathers together with the actual and dummy roots required. It has O((s+1)(l+1)) distinct computational labels. Use an invertible binary linear map to expose an injective hash into k₀=O(log(|U|+1)) front bits. A random k₀-bit projection separates U with positive probability by pair collision counting at k₀=ceil(2 log₂(|U|+1)); rank can be extended as needed. [Exact R91](PARALLEL_BILINEAR_WORD_QUERY.md) on these front bits XORs the unique marked tail into the remaining bits, using zero for absent bins. The resulting reversible address map sends every element of U to a distinct code with zero tail. It can send other words to unused code slots. Enlarge the code to k bits sufficient for a patch identifier, m local Haar bits, and all |U| genuine and dummy labels. Take k=max(k₀,ceil(log₂(s+1))+m+1). The additional leading bits of the tail are then part of the code; they are zero on U. Compute the zero test on the new remaining tail into the single supplied clean H using the earlier constant-depth full-operator equality circuit, with its actual inverse at the end. Choose its precision as a small fixed multiple of ε. Analyze the ideal complete test first. On H=1 all n−k tail bits are now conditionally available as zero, including at unused code slots. On H=0 they may be arbitrary. The compressed representation uses only the original logical qubits and returned dirty work.

For each pass choose a permutation π of the k-bit code space. Map every affected genuine or dummy label in its patch to (patch ID, local Haar input marker) in the m-bit complete local tree, using 0^m for the patch root. Complete the partial injection to a permutation. There are at most s patches and every virtual slot fits. On an auxiliary k-bit conditional zero register use the exact XOR of π, SWAP, and exact XOR of π^{-1} to enact (z,0)→(π(z),0). Both lookups are ordinary R91 tables on k bits with no private clean requirements. Its action with occupied auxiliary bits can be anything; the inverse is the reversed actual circuit and the inactive word cancels. After π, every local father and mother plane is represented literally in its own m-bit tree. All other canonical codes can be included in the circuit since the corresponding ideal product of local plane rotations fixes the orthogonal complement of these precise vectors.

## One antichain on the virtual output bits

Use the ordinary complete positive Haar on the m-bit local register, identically on every patch label. Its exact transformation carries a local father/mother rotation at local prefix w to the corresponding real rotation on the next local qubit supported on prefix w and suffix state |+...+>. This follows immediately by composing the remaining balanced levels on the zero detector after the elementary rotation. The immediate balanced rotation on the target commutes with the desired angular change.

In the output basis, compute the prefix-selection tag by R91 on (patch ID, all m data bits) into a conditional zero register of ceil(log₂(m+2)) bits. Use a sentinel when no marked prefix matches. In each patch the marked prefixes are incomparable, giving at most one match. For a match let tag j=|w|+1; membership depends only on its proper prefix and is preserved by any target operation on j and arbitrary suffix operations. From the short tag perform Hadamards on all positions strictly after the selected j; at sentinel perform none. All sites can run in parallel: precompute their predicates in separate conditional zero bits, apply controlled-H on separate data targets, and uncompute the predicates. The condition is a ≤ comparison on O(log m) bits whose exact addressed XOR has D=O(log log(m+2)) by R91; all such outputs can be computed by one complete word query. A fixed controlled-H has an exact constant-size Clifford+T circuit, e.g. H=V Z V† for a one-T conjugated Pauli rotation V, with a controlled Z in between. This basis change converts the selected suffix |+> condition into a zero condition.

Change only the selected jth target into the eigenbasis of Y, using per-site conditional Clifford basis changes obtained in the same way. The required diagonal operation acts only when H=1, patch and tag name a genuine defect, and the suffix after j consists of zeroes. Its desired eigenphase is ±δ_v, determined by the selected target bit. Quantize these arbitrary relative phases to O_{K,L}(1) fractional bits independently per orthogonal column; the single diagonal's error bound is independent of how many defects it covers. For each bit an exact R91 table keyed by (H, patch ID, data and short tag) writes its value to a conditional zero bit. Interleave its XOR and actual inverse with a relative one-site phase and its actual adjoint (the latter applied first) to obtain a literal phase on the active zero comparison. For H=0 the table is identically zero on the entire physical input including occupied private wires. Thus this pulse is exactly the identity there, for the actually realized one-site rotations including their global phases. Undo the eigenbasis change, the selected Hadamards and the tag recorder by their actual inverses. These are unitary conjugations, so even on inadequately initialized tags or scratch they cancel exactly when H=0. Undo the local Haar and the code relabeling. At H=1 all working clean bits are genuine conditional zeros; the active code is returned by the ideal action and errors from the known Haar implementation and all pulses telescope. On H=0 there is exact cancellation for the entire pass, including the actual local Haar and its inverse. Uncompute the full H test and the outer sparse address transformation. Apply the positive n-bit complete Haar for the final complete frame.

The R91 queries have combined address O(k+log m) and polynomial output arity in n and s, at most poly_{K,L}(s+2,n) table volume. At sufficient dirty allocation they have D=O(log(k+2))=O_{K,L}(1+log log(s+2)). The exceptional zero-tail test has O_{K,L}(log log(n+4)) depth by the following elementary variant, or O_{K,L}(1) with the stronger previously established equality circuit. A positive m-bit Haar has depth O(log(m+2)) already from R80; its one clean qubit is among the conditional zero bits and can be borrowed arbitrarily off the active sector. A total of O(k+m) conditional zero bits suffices in each pass for the permutation register, masks, tag, phase site and local Haar clean bit, while R91 and the Haar compiler use separate dirty registers. Each of the at most 2K passes is followed by its complete reversals where required. The final positive Haar on n bits has O_L(log log(n+4)) depth by the [complete positive Haar construction](POSITIVE_HAAR_FINGERPRINT.md). All histogram choices, local tables and permutations are finite prescribed preprocessing.

For a fixed K, the previous simpler proof for K exceptional levels follows as a special case, since every path has at most one node per level. The [sparse word dictionary theorem](SPARSE_WORD_AND_FRAME.md) is independently stronger in its square-root support count. This antichain application needs only its elementary 2 log|U| injective-projection branch.


### Elementary zero-tail guard using the existing Haar vote

At fixed tolerance τ and p tested bits, use M=2^h=Θ_τ((p+1)^2) candidate binary linear maps A_r from p bits to k=O_τ(log(p+2)) bits. Choose k so a random map kills a particular nonzero x with probability at most cτ/(4√M). The number of false-zero seeds at that x has mean at most cτ√M/4. The elementary binomial tail at cτ√M is bounded by exp(−c'τ√M). A sufficiently large constant in M makes the union over the 2^p−1 nonzero inputs less than one. Fix a good collection by finite search. At x=0 the seeded predicate [A_r x=0] is always correct. For a given r₀ its active term is a conjunction of h seed literals and k affine zero bits (and an optional binary probe). Compile all M terms on separate returned private dirty registers, sharing only read-only linear forms, and add their phase contributions. This has poly_τ(p) resources and O_τ(log log(p+4)) native depth. In each of the Z, X and Y product bases on the same h dirty seed bits the wrong projector has ≤cτ√M basis states. Its pairwise product with another wrong projector has operator norm ≤cτ by mutual unbiasedness and the Frobenius bound. The previously proved three-vote majority, actual unrecording and bounded-clean promotion therefore give a zero-clean complete XOR of the zero test at error τ, for sufficiently small c. The s-independent O(log log n) term in the final frame pays this cost.

## Sparse sign changes can be nested without a bound

The sign gauges in R80 also permit s_sign arbitrary signed edge changes at any depths for any fast magnitude frame. The output gauge due to a negative edge (v,b) is the prefix predicate [x starts vb]. For the input gauge let d=|v| and t be the position of the last one in x, with t=0 on the zero string. The edge contributes when t>d+1 and x starts vb, or when t=d+1 with prefix v. Its parity expression is therefore
```math
 [x\text{ starts }vb]\;\oplus\;[x=vb0^{n-d-1}]\;\oplus\;[x=v1 0^{n-d-1}].
```
This follows from the ancestor sign product for the input mother marker and the two edge signs at its own birth node. The formula handles the root and either sign choice on zero edges under the R80 conventions. Thus each negative edge adds at most three Boolean conjunctions to each gauge. Give every term its own read-only approximate cube-sign circuit at error ε/O(s_sign+1). The preceding three-basis linear hash proof works quantitatively: M=poly(n+2,1/τ), h,k=O(log(n+2)+log(1/τ)), and all exact predicates use O(h+k) affine control bits. The native conjunction depth is O(log(h+k+2)) and three-basis vote and bounded-clean promotion take only a constant number of chronological oracle calls. Since the construction only reads tested bits as Clifford controls, all cube-sign circuits run simultaneously on disjoint private wires. Their full-error total is bounded by the sum; the ideal phases commute even on coherent inputs. The additional total counts and dirty width are polynomial in n+s_sign+1 at fixed L, with depth O_L(1+log(log(n+2)+log(s_sign+2))). This allows any number of nested sign defects, together with the bounded nesting needed for unrestricted angle magnitudes. With s_sign polynomial in n it preserves the doubly logarithmic bound.
