## 2. Gaussian compression: include the signs and all arbitrary work

The [Gaussian compression theorem](GAUSSIAN_COMPRESSION_SOURCE_LIMITS.md)
allows arbitrary fixed Cliffords on both carrier banks, arbitrary dirty
width and a k-qubit initialized signal. The signal need not return before
compression. The address is read-only, and each fixed-address physical
fiber is Clifford.

If a complete compression approximates a unitary divided by square root
of two with error less than half of square-root-two minus one, its
singular values force exact rank-one clean overlap. The normalized
compression is exactly Clifford. Thus flatness is proved in this grammar,
and the retained paid recovery returns the signal exactly; it is not
inferred from one favorable input column.

For physical tableau-label degree D and generator-sign degree E, the
logical labels have degree at most 2kD and the logical signs have degree
at most E+(2k+4)D, independently of dirty width. For q literal affine
XOR queries separated by fixed bank Cliffords, D is at most q and E is
at most 3q. Therefore inverse-column transport requires 2kq at least p,
and direct indicator compression requires (2k+7)q at least p. The latter
statement tests an output Pauli sign and does not mistake the indicator's
identity symplectic matrix for absence of address dependence.

The proof home states the extra allowance for read-only global Clifford
boundaries. Address Hadamards, genuine non-Clifford bank dynamics and
joint native resynthesis remain outside this query-stage result. In
particular, the stage inequality cannot be substituted for a native
T-depth lower bound.

This resolves the proposed Gaussian pivot shortcut negatively, even with
Hadamards on both banks and a small clean compression. Paying more fixed
Gaussian interlayers does not supply the desired sublogarithmic source
in that retained schedule. A new construction must change the mechanism.

## 3. Scalar interference: a Gauss sum is not automatically a source

The [involutory Gauss analysis](INVOLUTORY_GAUSS_SOURCE_LIMITS.md)
keeps every carrier wire arbitrary. A diagonal Clifford quadratic phase
followed by a full Fourier transform has an exact third-power identity:
a normalized Gauss scalar times an in-place matrix map and two literal
Pauli corrections. The sixth power returns all carrier wires, but squares
the Gauss scalar. For an affine symmetric involution, the resulting
Boolean sign has degree at most three. The doubled-block trial computes
trace parity, not rank parity.

The unsquared scalar initially looked more promising. Its correction
would require the actual in-place map; three forward XOR queries pay
only a paired map on two arbitrary banks. The stronger revision now
precedes that compilation question: if the symmetric involutory matrix
entries have Boolean degree at most D on the full external input cube,
a Gauss scalar that is a fixed eighth root times a Boolean sign has
sign degree at most 9D. The proof uses the characteristic Gauss formula
and determinant expansion modulo sixteen, not a numerical fit.

The addressed phase has degree p+1 in address and output-bank bits.
Consequently bounded-degree involutory coefficients cannot supply it
for growing p, even if the in-place correction were granted for free.
This restriction includes output-bank variables treated as external
coefficients, a possibility not covered by a label-only Gaussian screen.
A promise that the bank is one-hot or initialized would abandon the
complete operator contract and the theorem's full-cube premise.

General non-involutory forms are outside this result. Their inverse or
whole-word completion must still be supplied and charged. Reintroducing
the old inverse-column source without paying that operation retains the
old cost. The exact loop ledgers and approximate telescoping errors are
recorded in the proof home; neither an uncomputed Gauss sign nor an
initialized-carrier overlap is counted as a native operation.

## 4. The original-pair scout: prove transport before classifying it

The [center-transport revision](FOUR_BAND_CENTER_TRANSPORT.md) uses
actual independent signed native lists and the existing physical clean
code. It strengthens understanding of the fixed-endpoint examples;
it does not assert a universal four-band degree bound.

First, a literal repartition of the controlled-SWAP example gives a
non-Clifford intermediate probe while retaining exact, nontrivial complete
Boolean action. This is a concrete counterexample to inferring initial
B matching from complete return. Its displayed four-band, fourteen-T
word still has the previously proved two-band, eight-T realization.
The failed inference is real; extra optimal depth does not follow.

Second, one original pair-orientation mismatch with the same original
endpoint probes still restores common-probe matching after the actual
additional-band repair. The fixed-endpoint cubic closure applies.
Merely absorbing a repair into a renamed endpoint does not establish a
new physical mechanism.

Third, a sole unmatched axis with anticommuting probe image is forced
to meet its inverse literal T rotation in the added band, under the
stated physical class. The proof uses genuine center-sector codes and
only invokes native independence back on the full physical space.
The scalar is retained when the two gates are removed.

Finally, the preserved center is itself derived from occupied Booleanity
in this fixed-endpoint class. Its logical pullback is computationally
diagonal; the actual added-band image is Clifford; clean-code rigidity
then forces literal center preservation. No promise is imposed on the
unused clean block. Genuinely different endpoint probes or a physical
center with non-diagonal logical pullback lie outside this argument.
Those are substantive center-transport questions. Multiple unmatched
B factors also remain open with the same endpoints; the results do not
classify that whole class or certify depth by counting displayed features.

For general endpoint probes, the actual original pair still supplies a
clean-preserving physical Pauli. Conjugating its center through the added
band gives an actual Clifford. The resulting Boolean directional
derivative has a paid two-band implementation using the original output
endpoint, with exact return and no additional work. Its degree is at
most three. This constrains only that direction: high-degree terms
invariant under the translation remain possible, and a diagonal center
gives a vacuous derivative. It is not a four-band total-degree theorem.
For the complete addressed phase and more than three address bits, it
forces every such center's logical X mask to leave the address fixed
and to translate the bank by the truth table of a cubic Boolean
function. This constrains the projected masks, not the number of
independent physical centers.

## 5. What remains to be paid

The main missing operation is still a complete shallow source, with
all arbitrary output, dirty and reference inputs included. The paid
amplitude recovery, promotion and fusion do not supply that source.

Two constructive questions remain distinct. A crossed non-Gaussian
interference word must prove its actual collision, amplitude and return
identity before an inverse-looking kernel can be used as readout. A
native parallel promotion of a larger clean signal would need to pay
coherent control sharing on arbitrary dirty inputs; the exponential
black-box call count alone is not a native-depth obstruction. Neither
construction has been supplied by the present results.

All new limits are stated with their exact representation hypotheses.
The retained general resource formulas, error thresholds and return
recurrences are unchanged. The next mechanism should be judged by its
complete physical action and simultaneous ledger, not by the appearance
of a matrix inverse, a Gauss sum, or an additional native band.
