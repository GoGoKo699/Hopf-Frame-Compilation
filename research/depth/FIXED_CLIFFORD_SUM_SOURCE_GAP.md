## 1. Complete source theorem and its scope

Fix a number k of clean source inputs. Let J insert those inputs in zero.
Let U_A and U_B
be actual one-band native encoders on all wires, and put

```math
K=J^\dagger U_B^\dagger U_AJ.
```

All other inputs are arbitrary: the p address wires, the complete
$`m=2^p`$ output bank, and every private dirty-work wire. The target is
the complete indicator I_p, tensored with identity on private work.
There are constants $`\tau_0(k)>0`$ and $`m_0(k)`$, independent of
every source width, gate count and positive amplitude, such that for
every $`0\lt\alpha\le1`$

```math
m\ge m_0(k)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\|K-\alpha I_p\|>\tau_0(k)\alpha.
```

The same conclusion holds after optimizing a common source phase.
The norm is the operator norm of the complete compressed logical
block, including every address, output and dirty-work column. It is
unchanged by tensoring an arbitrary external reference.
It requires neither exact nor approximately rounded flatness.

Consequently the two-one-band-source interface cannot provide the
complete indicator to every fixed positive **relative** accuracy as the
address size grows. Increasing the source's gate counts or width, or
letting its amplitude decrease with address size, cannot evade this
statement. In particular it excludes the proposed one-clean source at
amplitude $`1/\sqrt2`$, even without imposing its desired linear
T-count/dirty-width and quadratic Clifford-count budgets.

This is a source theorem. It does not give an asymptotic lower bound
on unrestricted indicator T-depth, and it does not exclude a different
constant-depth mechanism. The theorem fixes an upper bound k_0 on the
clean count before taking the address size large. Any source
with k≤k_0 is covered by padding it with k_0−k idle clean wires, which
leaves its compressed block unchanged. Thus bounded variation of the
clean count is already covered. The fixed-sum argument itself supplies
no uniform constants for unbounded clean counts. Section 10 supplies a stronger exact
growing-clean bound and explicit finite-error screens by a separate
Clifford rank--norm argument. The earlier fixed-amplitude proof and its
useful constants are retained in Sections 2--6; the amplitude-free
defect in Section 7 proves the stronger statement above. Section 8 also
gives a finite-size lower bound on every nonzero one-signal compression.
There is no amplitude-independent absolute error floor: as alpha tends
to zero, the zero block has absolute error alpha. Such a block contains
no approximate normalized indicator.

The full conditional promotion and R98 recurrences remain correct in
their [existing home](DIRTY_PROMOTION_OF_CLEAN_QUERIES.md#61-exact-flatness-sharpens-the-complete-error-and-return-recurrence).
This theorem stops this source interface from supplying their
arbitrary-accuracy indicator premise. It does not change R96.

## 2. Fixed-length literal Clifford sums have a uniform gap

For an operator on q qubits, use normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm

```math
\|E\|_{2,q}^2=2^{-q}\mathrm{tr}(E^\dagger E).
```

Here a literal Clifford means an element of the group generated by
the native H, S and CX gates, including its actual global phase.

**Lemma.** Fix M and a finite set of complex coefficients F. There is
a positive constant g(M,F), independent of q, such that every nonzero
sum of at most M literal q-qubit Cliffords with coefficients in F has
normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm at least g(M,F).

The coefficients must remain fixed. Allowing an arbitrarily small
coefficient makes the claim false. Allowing an arbitrary continuous
phase on each Clifford also makes it false: the norm of
$`I-e^{i\theta}I`$ tends to zero with theta. A common phase on a source
is different: it cancels from the quadratic defect used below.

### 2.1. Exact overlaps and their geometric distance

The normalized Choi vector of a q-qubit literal Clifford is a
stabilizer state on N=2q qubits. Let Lambda_i be its N-dimensional
stabilizer label space, with Pauli signs omitted, and define

```math
r_{ij}=N-\dim(\Lambda_i\cap\Lambda_j).
```

Taking the trace of the two stabilizer-state projectors shows that
their inner product is either zero, when the common labels have
incompatible eigenvalue signs, or has modulus $`2^{-r_{ij}/2}`$.
Moreover

```math
r_{ik}\le r_{ij}+r_{jk}.
```

Indeed the intersection of $`\Lambda_i\cap\Lambda_j`$ and
$`\Lambda_j\cap\Lambda_k`$ has dimension at least the sum of their
dimensions minus N, and is contained in
$`\Lambda_i\cap\Lambda_k`$.

The literal phase is also discrete. Each matrix entry of a native
Clifford word with h Hadamards belongs to
$`2^{-h/2}\mathbb Z[i]`$. A Choi overlap is a matrix entry of such a
word: prepare a Bell state, apply the relative Clifford, and undo the
Bell preparation. For a nonzero overlap its Gaussian-integer numerator
therefore has norm $`2^{h-r_{ij}}`$. A Gaussian integer with
power-of-two norm is a unit times a power of $`1+i`$, so its phase is
an eighth root of unity. Thus

```math
\langle C_i|C_j\rangle=0
\quad\text{or}\quad
\zeta_8^u2^{-r_{ij}/2},\qquad \zeta_8=e^{i\pi/4}.
```

This establishes the phase convention directly from the native
alphabet; it does not choose unrelated representatives of projective
Cliffords.

### 2.2. Proof of the uniform gap

Suppose a sequence of nonzero sums, with a fixed coefficient list,
has norm tending to zero. By taking a subsequence, every entry of its
M-by-M Gram matrix converges. Join two indices when their limiting
overlap is nonzero.

Along an edge, the distance r is bounded. The triangle inequality
therefore bounds r for every pair in a connected component. All Gram
entries within that component consequently belong to a fixed finite
set, including zero. On a further subsequence those entries are
exactly constant. Entries between different components tend to zero.

The limiting squared norm of the full sum is the sum of the
nonnegative squared norms of the component sums. Every component sum
has zero limiting norm. Its internal Gram matrix is already exactly
constant, so that component sum is exactly zero at every sufficiently
late point in the subsequence. The whole sum is then exactly zero,
contradicting the initial assumption. A finite coefficient set gives
only finitely many coefficient lists, proving the lemma.

The zero-overlap case is essential here. Its unsigned distance need
not be large, but bounded distance still confines the overlap to a
finite set. No growing-rank alphabet has been declared finite.

## 3. An explicit, deliberately conservative integer bound

For integer coefficients of absolute value at most an integer B≥1,
the gap can be made fully explicit. Put a_1=0 and, for j≥2, define

```math
b_j=\lceil\log_2(jB)\rceil,\qquad
R_j=4a_{j-1}+4b_j+2,
```

```math
a_j=(j-1)R_j/2+1+b_j.
```

Then every nonzero sum of at most M such terms has norm at least
$`2^{-a_M}`$. Zero coefficients may be removed.

**Proof.** A nonzero singleton has norm at least one. Induct on the
number j of terms, and join two terms when their overlap magnitude is
at least $`2^{-R_j/2}`$.

If this graph is disconnected, each component has a proper sublist.
If all component sums vanish, the entire sum vanishes. Otherwise the
sum of the component squared norms is at least
$`2^{-2a_{j-1}}`$. All cross-component ordered pairs together have
absolute contribution at most

```math
j^2B^2 2^{-R_j/2}\le2^{-2a_{j-1}-1}.
```

The full sum therefore has norm at least
$`2^{-a_{j-1}-1/2}`$, which is larger than $`2^{-a_j}`$.

If the graph is connected, all pairwise unsigned distances are at
most D=(j−1)R_j. Set $`h=\lfloor D/2\rfloor+1`$. Every real Gram
entry lies in $`2^{-h}\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]`$. Its image under the
conjugation $`\sqrt2\mapsto-\sqrt2`$ has the same absolute value:
each such entry is zero, a signed power of two, or a signed power of
two times square root of two.

Consequently the squared norm x of the integer-coefficient sum lies
in this same ring, and its algebraic conjugate obeys
$`|x'|\le j^2B^2`$. If x is nonzero, its algebraic norm has absolute
value at least $`2^{-2h}`$. Hence

```math
x\ge\frac{2^{-2h}}{j^2B^2},\qquad
\left\|\sum_i c_iC_i\right\|_{2,q}
\ge\frac{2^{-h}}{jB}\ge2^{-a_j}.
```

For M=10 and B=2, the recurrence gives

```math
a_{10}=1\,280\,499\,909.
```

This is an explicit existence bound, not a practical threshold. No
claim of sharpness is made.

## 4. The direct projected defect

Let q denote the number of nonsignal wires. Fix any original output
Pauli P=Z_j, extended by identity on the k clean wires and every
other wire. Define

```math
\Pi_A=U_AJJ^\dagger U_A^\dagger,\qquad
\Pi_B=U_BJJ^\dagger U_B^\dagger,
```

```math
C=U_B P U_B^\dagger,\qquad D=U_A P U_A^\dagger.
```

A one-band encoder sends every Pauli to a literal Clifford. In
particular, for the clean-register Paulis $`Z^s`$,

```math
A_s=U_AZ^sU_A^\dagger,\qquad
B_t=U_BZ^tU_B^\dagger
```

are literal Cliffords, as are C and D. This assertion preserves the
actual phase: conjugating a Pauli through each native T or TDG gives
an actual Clifford, and all outside native Cliffords remain literal.
The scalar eighth roots needed in this identity are native as well,
since $`(HS)^3=\zeta_8 I`$.
The exact projector expansions are

```math
\Pi_A=2^{-k}\sum_s A_s,\qquad
\Pi_B=2^{-k}\sum_t B_t.
```

Consider the full physical operator

```math
X=\Pi_A\Pi_B C\Pi_A-\alpha^2D\Pi_A.
```

C commutes with Pi_B, and D commutes with Pi_A. Thus X is supported
on the A code on both sides. Its exact restriction is

```math
J^\dagger U_A^\dagger XU_AJ=K^\dagger P K-\alpha^2P.
```

This follows by inserting the definitions of Pi_B and C; the middle
clean projector commutes with the original output P. Every logical,
output and dirty input is retained. Since X vanishes on the
orthogonal complement of this code,

```math
\|X\|_{2,q+k}
=2^{-k/2}\|K^\dagger P K-\alpha^2P\|_{2,q}.
```

Expanding the projectors expresses X as a sum of

```math
M_k=2^{3k}+2^k
```

literal Cliffords, with the fixed coefficients
$`2^{-3k}`$ and $`-\alpha^2 2^{-k}`$. The lemma therefore gives a
positive number g=g(k,alpha), independent of q and all source
resources, for which

```math
X=0\quad\text{or}\quad\|X\|_{2,q+k}\ge g.
```

The finite sum is used as a mathematical invariant. No sum of
Cliffords is treated as a supplied native operation.

## 5. Sparse indicator probes force a full-operator contradiction

Suppose $`\|K-\alpha I_p\|\le\epsilon`$. Quadratic expansion gives

```math
\|K^\dagger P K-\alpha^2 I_p^\dagger P I_p\|
\le2\alpha\epsilon+\epsilon^2.
```

For the original output wire j, the indicator changes the sign of P
on exactly the address sector x=j. This sector has relative
dimension 1/m, including all output and dirty inputs. Therefore

```math
\|I_p^\dagger P I_p-P\|_{2,q}=2/\sqrt m,
\qquad
\|I_p^\dagger P I_p-P\|=2.
```

Using normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm bounded by operator norm,

```math
\|X\|_{2,q+k}
\le2^{-k/2}
\left(2\alpha\epsilon+\epsilon^2+\frac{2\alpha^2}{\sqrt m}\right).
```

Choose

```math
\epsilon_0=
\min\left\{\frac\alpha4,\frac{2^{k/2}g}{8\alpha}\right\},
\qquad
m_0=\max\left\{1,\frac{64\alpha^4 2^{-k}}{g^2}\right\}.
```

For epsilon≤epsilon_0 and m≥m_0, the first two terms contribute at
most 9g/32 and the last contributes at most g/4. Thus

```math
\|X\|_{2,q+k}\le17g/32\lt g.
```

The gap forces X=0 and hence $`K^\dagger P K=\alpha^2P`$. But the
full operator distance of this probe from the indicator probe is
$`2\alpha^2`$, whereas its assumed approximation error is at most

```math
2\alpha\epsilon+\epsilon^2
\le9\alpha^2/16\lt2\alpha^2.
```

This contradiction proves the theorem. The proof uses a normalized
trace only to force an exact algebraic identity; its final witness is
the original output operator on the full address sector x=j. It does
not replace the operator contract by an average-case approximation.

## 6. One clean signal at amplitude one over square root of two

For k=1 and $`\alpha=1/\sqrt2`$, write
$`A=U_AZ_aU_A^\dagger`$ and $`B=U_BZ_aU_B^\dagger`$. The direct
defect has the explicit expansion

```math
8X=(I+A)(I+B)C(I+A)-2D(I+A).
```

It contains eight terms with coefficient +1 and two with coefficient
−2. No commutation between A and B, anticommutator identity, or
flatness assumption is used. The integer bound gives the valid choice

```math
g=2^{-a_{10}-3},\qquad
\epsilon_0=g/4,\qquad m_0=8/g^2.
```

Equivalently a conservative address threshold is

```math
p_0=2a_{10}+9=2\,560\,999\,827.
```

The enormous displayed threshold records a constructive proof of a
positive asymptotic floor. It gives no useful estimate of the first
excluded small address size. This threshold belongs to the fixed-sum
proof. The separate rank--norm argument in Section 10 excludes exact one-clean sources
already at p at least 18, and relative error at most 1/1024 at p at
least 20. Neither calculation asserts an unrestricted depth bound.

The earlier parity-copy screen, scalar-commuting-probe screen, and
computed signal-mixing family retain their exact finite-size scopes
in [their proof home](OVERLAPPING_TWO_BAND_SOURCES.md). Their examples
also explain why replacing the transported four-term unitary by a
Clifford was not a valid shortcut. The direct defect closes the
general source interface by a different invariant; it does not
assert that those transported unitaries are Cliffords.

## 7. An amplitude-free defect gives a uniform relative gap

Allow the positive amplitude alpha to depend arbitrarily on address
size, requested accuracy or the source word, while retaining
$`0\lt\alpha\le1`$. Keep k bounded in advance. The coefficient
$`-\alpha^2 2^{-k}`$ in Section 4 would no longer be fixed, so that
particular gap cannot simply be reused uniformly. Instead compare the
transported output probe with the source's own compressed Gram operator.

Use the same actual encoders, projectors, C and D as in Section 4 and
define

```math
X_{\mathrm{rel}}
 =\Pi_A\Pi_B C\Pi_A-D\Pi_A\Pi_B\Pi_A.
```

Put $`V_A=U_AJ`$ and $`V_B=U_BJ`$. They are isometries onto
their respective full code spaces, and $`K=V_B^\dagger V_A`$.
Since the original output P commutes with the initialized clean
projector, direct insertion gives the exact identity

```math
X_{\mathrm{rel}}
 =V_A\bigl(K^\dagger P K-PK^\dagger K\bigr)V_A^\dagger.
```

In particular X_rel is supported on the A code on both sides, without
any flatness or return premise. With q nonsignal wires,

```math
\|X_{\mathrm{rel}}\|_{2,q+k}
 =2^{-k/2}\|K^\dagger P K-PK^\dagger K\|_{2,q}.
```

Expanding its three projectors in each term gives at most

```math
M_k^{\mathrm{rel}}=2^{3k+1}
```

literal Cliffords, all with coefficients in
$`\{2^{-3k},-2^{-3k}\}`$. These coefficients do not involve alpha.
Repeated terms may be kept as separate summands. The fixed-sum lemma
therefore supplies $`g_k>0`$, independent of source width and gate
count, such that

```math
X_{\mathrm{rel}}=0
\quad\hbox{or}\quad
\|X_{\mathrm{rel}}\|_{2,q+k}\ge g_k.
```

For an explicit conservative choice, apply Section 3 with B=1 and
$`M=M_k^{\mathrm{rel}}`$ to the integer-coefficient sum
$`2^{3k}X_{\mathrm{rel}}`$. It gives
$`g_k=2^{-3k-a_M}`$. No sharpness is claimed.

Suppose now that the complete block obeys the relative estimate

```math
\|K-\alpha I_p\|\le\tau\alpha.
```

Write $`K=\alpha I_p+E`$. The two quadratic expressions have
errors at most $`2\alpha\|E\|+\|E\|^2`$ each. Hence

```math
\begin{aligned}
\bigl\|K^\dagger P K-PK^\dagger K
 -\alpha^2(I_p^\dagger P I_p-P)\bigr\|
 &\le\alpha^2(4\tau+2\tau^2),\\
\|X_{\mathrm{rel}}\|_{2,q+k}
 &\le2^{-k/2}\alpha^2
       \left(4\tau+2\tau^2+\frac2{\sqrt m}\right).
\end{aligned}
```

The second line uses the same sparse output-probe witness as Section 5;
it is a normalized-trace estimate for the exact defect, while the
source premise remains the full operator norm. Choose

```math
\begin{aligned}
\tau_0(k)&=\min\left\{\frac18,
                         \frac{2^{k/2}g_k}{16}\right\},\\
m_0(k)&=\max\left\{1,\frac{64\,2^{-k}}{g_k^2}\right\}.
\end{aligned}
```

For $`\tau\le\tau_0(k)`$ and $`m\ge m_0(k)`$, one has
$`4\tau+2\tau^2\le17\tau/4`$. Since alpha is at most one,
the two contributions to the last bound are at most 17g_k/64 and
g_k/4, respectively. Thus

```math
\|X_{\mathrm{rel}}\|_{2,q+k}\le\frac{33}{64}g_k\lt g_k.
```

The gap forces X_rel to vanish. Its exact code restriction then says
$`K^\dagger P K=PK^\dagger K`$. The complete indicator witness,
however, has operator norm $`2\alpha^2`$, so the relative error
bound would require

```math
2\alpha^2
 \le\alpha^2(4\tau+2\tau^2)
 \le\frac{17}{32}\alpha^2.
```

This is impossible for every positive alpha. The constants are uniform
over its entire interval, proving Section 1's stronger theorem.
An arbitrary common source phase cancels from both quadratic terms,
so the result also holds after optimizing that phase.

The proof is an invariant calculation, not a native implementation of
X_rel. It retains the complete bank, arbitrary dirty inputs and reference
entanglement, and uses the actual encoder adjoints. It assumes neither
exact nor approximate clean return beyond the stated compressed block.
Padding a source with idle clean wires covers any clean count at most
a fixed k_0 by the constants for k_0.

This closes decreasing-amplitude two-band sources at uniformly bounded
clean count as a way to supply arbitrarily small fixed relative error.
It does not exclude every modest numerical tolerance: the displayed
constants remain conservative. Nor does it provide constants uniform
in unbounded k, whether k grows with address size or with the requested
accuracy. Additional source bands and unrestricted constant-depth
circuits remain outside this theorem. The polynomial-overhead fusion
does not alter the conclusion, because no count or width hypothesis was
used here.

## 8. A sharp finite-size norm gap for one clean signal

There is a separate useful obstruction before invoking a large-address
indicator witness. For one clean signal and the same actual one-band
encoders, let

```math
A=U_AZ_aU_A^\dagger,\qquad B=U_BZ_aU_B^\dagger.
```

Both are Hermitian literal Cliffords, and their positive eigenspaces
each have half the full physical dimension. Then

```math
\|K\|=\|\Pi_B\Pi_A\|=\frac12\|A+B\|.
```

The first equality follows by writing
$`\Pi_B\Pi_A=V_BKV_A^\dagger`$. For the second, write the
unitary $`U_B^\dagger U_A`$ in its two equally sized signal blocks,
with diagonal blocks K and L. Its square off-diagonal blocks and
unitarity imply that $`K^\dagger K`$ and $`L^\dagger L`$ have
the same spectrum. In U_A coordinates, $`(A+B)^2`$ is block
diagonal with blocks $`4K^\dagger K`$ and $`4L^\dagger L`$.
Taking norms proves the identity. The equal half-dimensional sectors
are essential to this step.

If K is nonzero, A and minus B are distinct Hermitian Cliffords. Their
normalized Choi states either differ by sign, giving operator distance
two, or are distinct projective stabilizer states, whose overlap has
magnitude at most $`1/\sqrt2`$. Consequently

```math
K=0\quad\hbox{or}\quad
\|K\|\ge\frac{\sqrt{2-\sqrt2}}2=\sin(\pi/8).
```

This constant is sharp: take $`U_A=I`$ and
$`U_B=T_{Y_a}X_a`$, with identity on any spectators. Then A is Z,
B is minus H, and the nonzero scalar compressed block has magnitude
$`\sin(\pi/8)`$. Taking $`U_B=X_a`$ instead gives B equal to
minus A and K equal to zero. Both are actual native sources; literal
scalar phases are retained when evaluating their compressed blocks.

For any unitary target V, not just an indicator, a meaningful relative
estimate with $`\|K-\alpha V\|\le\tau\alpha`$ and
$`\tau\lt1`$ excludes K equal to zero. It therefore requires

```math
\alpha\ge\frac{\sin(\pi/8)}{1+\tau}.
```

Unlike Section 7's indicator theorem, this finite-size conclusion
needs only one signal and no large address. It bounds the norm of the
whole compression, not every nonzero singular value and not the
compression of a general deeper circuit. It does not extend the
half-sector norm identity to several clean signals.

The [two focused exact fixtures](../../tests/test_relative_amplitude_source_gap.py)
check the amplitude-free physical/code defect identity, its literal
projector expansion, actual adjoints and common-phase cancellation, and
the sharp one-signal norm example with its zero-block branch. The uniform
relative gap and all-size scope remain analytic.

## 9. Exact growing-clean sources and the shrinking approximate threshold

The retained fixed-sum recurrence gives the following quantitative
exact corollary. Section 10 strengthens it by a separate rank--norm
argument. Both concern the same two actual one-band encoders and
their complete compressed indicator block, not unrestricted depth.
For B equal to one, write

```math
a_1=0,\qquad
a_j=2(j-1)a_{j-1}+(2j-1)\lceil\log_2j\rceil+j.
```

Dividing by the homogeneous multiplier and summing yields

```math
\begin{aligned}
a_M&=2^{M-1}(M-1)!S_M,\\
S_M&=\sum_{j=2}^M
\frac{(2j-1)\lceil\log_2j\rceil+j}{2^{j-1}(j-1)!}.
\end{aligned}
```

For M at least two, its first term gives $`S_M\ge5/2`$.
Using $`\lceil\log_2j\rceil\le j-1`$ bounds the full series by
$`(7/2)e^{1/2}-1\lt5`$. Consequently
$`\log_2 a_M=M\log_2M+O(M)`$.

Suppose the complete block is exactly $`K=I_p/\sqrt2`$, up to one
common scalar phase, with k initialized signals and every other input
arbitrary. For an original output Z probe, Section 7's nonzero relative
defect has normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm $`2^{-(p+k)/2}`$.
Its literal Clifford expansion has $`M=2^{3k+1}`$ terms and
coefficients of magnitude $`2^{-3k}`$. The explicit gap requires

```math
2^{-(p+k)/2}\ge2^{-3k-a_M},\qquad p\le2a_M+5k.
```

Inverting the displayed growth of a_M gives the necessary condition

```math
k\ge\frac13\log_2\log_2p
 -\frac13\log_2\log_2\log_2p-O(1).
```

Thus $`k=o(\log\log p)`$ cannot supply an exact two-band source.
The [paid parallel-promotion window](PARALLEL_CLEAN_PROMOTION.md#51-paying-any-sufficiently-small-packing-permutation)
is broader: it includes k asymptotic to beta times log log p for every
fixed beta below one. By itself this exact corollary leaves, for example,
$`1/3\le\beta\lt1`$ unresolved. Section 10 closes the larger exact
$`k=o(\log p)`$ window; the growing-clean constant-accuracy source
question remains open.

The approximate conclusion is different. The same proof supplies

```math
\tau_0(k)=2^{-a_M-(5/2)k-4},\qquad
p\ge2a_M+5k+6.
```

This excluded relative tolerance tends to zero with k. Even an error
of order $`2^{-k}`$ can greatly exceed it. Hence the calculation does
not exclude fixed-positive-error sources with growing clean count,
nor give a uniform fixed-error obstruction in the paid packing window.
Budgets promised separately at every fixed accuracy cannot be used at
this shrinking tolerance without a uniform precision estimate. This
distinction also prevents a fixed-k theorem from excluding every
accuracy-dependent but address-independent allocation k(tau).

## 10. Rank-norm bounds sharpen the exact and finite-error source screen

The exact conclusion of Section 9 can be strengthened from order
log log p clean inputs to order log p. The amplitude and all dirty
widths remain unrestricted. The approximate conclusion below has a
shrinking accuracy threshold and does not exclude constant-accuracy
sources throughout the growing-clean promotion window.

### Operator norm versus normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm

For an arbitrary n-qubit operator E, let $`\chi_{\mathrm C}(E)`$
be the minimum number of Clifford unitaries in a complex linear
combination equal to E. Let $`\Lambda_{\mathrm C}(E)`$ be the
minimum coefficient l1 norm over all such decompositions. The
triangle inequality gives $`\|E\|\le\Lambda_{\mathrm C}(E)`$.
Global Clifford phases can be absorbed into the coefficients; every
literal Clifford used in this note is an admissible summand.

[Sinha and Lovitz, arXiv:2610.06819v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06819v1),
Theorem 4.7 and Corollary 4.9, prove the dimension-independent bound

```math
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{\mathrm C}(E)\le M
&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\|E\|^2\le\Lambda_{\mathrm C}(E)^2
       \le B(M)\|E\|_{2,n}^2,\\
B(M)&=\min\left\{\frac{4^M-1}{3},
              2^{12\sqrt{M\log_2(2M)}}\right\}.
\end{aligned}
```

This is the operator theorem, including nonunitary E, with
$`\|E\|_{2,n}^2=2^{-n}\mathrm{tr}(E^\dagger E)`$.
It permits arbitrary complex coefficients. The second bound uses
the paper's weighted clustering theorem; no fixed-coefficient gap
or unencoded-target assumption is substituted for it.

For completeness, the first bound has the following elementary
one-term elimination proof. Under the normalized Choi map,
Cliffords correspond exactly to stabilizer states maximally
entangled across the n:n cut. Write $`\Lambda_{\mathrm M}`$
for coefficient l1 norm over those states. A stabilizer missing a
ebits from maximal entanglement has
$`\Lambda_{\mathrm M}\le2^{a/2}`$: its bipartite stabilizer
normal form has a pairs with no entanglement, each expanded as the
sum of two Bell states with coefficient $`1/\sqrt2`$.

A nonzero Pauli projection increases this deficit by at most one.
Indeed, a nondeterministic stabilizer measurement replaces one
stabilizer generator; the old and new label spaces share a
codimension-one subspace. Their intersections with the left-local
Pauli space therefore differ in dimension by at most one. That
dimension is the stabilizer's entanglement deficit. Deterministic
nonzero projections do not change the state.

Induct on t for a vector v expressed with t stabilizers of deficit
at most a. Choose one displayed stabilizer s, put
$`P_s=|s\rangle\langle s|`$, and average
$`Q_g=(I-g)/2`$ over its full signed stabilizer group. Then

```math
v=P_sv+2\mathbb E_g Q_gv,\qquad
\mathbb E_g\|Q_gv\|^2=\tfrac12\|(I-P_s)v\|^2.
```

Every $`Q_gv`$ has at most t minus one surviving stabilizer
summands and deficit at most a+1. If their squared coefficient
norm is bounded by $`2^{a+1}c_{t-1}`$ times their squared norm,
the triangle and Cauchy--Schwarz inequalities give

```math
\begin{aligned}
\Lambda_{\mathrm M}(v)
&\le2^{a/2}\left(\|P_sv\|
           +2\sqrt{c_{t-1}}\|(I-P_s)v\|\right),\\
\Lambda_{\mathrm M}(v)^2
&\le2^a(1+4c_{t-1})\|v\|^2.
\end{aligned}
```

Thus $`c_0=0`$ and $`c_t=1+4c_{t-1}=(4^t-1)/3`$.
Apply the deficit-zero case to a Clifford Choi decomposition of E.
Its norm squared is $`\|E\|_{2,n}^2`$, proving the first
operator bound without the stronger clustering result. This
weighted argument retains maximal-entanglement costs; a generic
stabilizer-state extent bound alone would not suffice.

### Exact complete sources require logarithmically many clean inputs

Use the direct defect from Section 4, not the larger relative defect:

```math
X=\Pi_A\Pi_B C\Pi_A-\alpha^2D\Pi_A,
\qquad M_k=2^{3k}+2^k.
```

Its displayed literal Clifford expansion proves
$`\chi_{\mathrm C}(X)\le M_k`$ even when alpha varies, because
the rank--norm theorem does not require fixed coefficients. If
$`K=\alpha I_p`$ exactly, with any positive alpha, the original
single-output Pauli probe gives

```math
\|X\|=2\alpha^2,\qquad
\|X\|_{2,q+k}=2\alpha^2\,2^{-(p+k)/2}.
```

The factor $`2^{-p/2}`$ is the one-address support of the defect;
$`2^{-k/2}`$ is the normalized-trace conversion from the full
A code to the physical space. Every output and private dirty column
is included in these norms. The operator inequality now gives

```math
\boxed{\quad
p+k\le\log_2 B(M_k)
\le12\sqrt{M_k\log_2(2M_k)}.
\quad}
```

Since $`M_k\le2^{3k+1}`$, inversion yields

```math
k\ge\frac23\log_2p
       -\frac13\log_2\log_2p-O(1).
```

Even the elementary bound alone gives
$`p+k\lt2M_k\le2^{3k+2}`$, hence
$`k\gt(\log_2p-2)/3`$. Thus exact two-one-band sources with
$`k=o(\log p)`$ cannot supply the complete indicator at any
positive amplitude, including $`1/\sqrt2`$. This closes the exact
source part of the larger conditional promotion window.

For one clean signal, $`M_1=10`$. The elementary inequality gives
$`p+1\le\log_2((4^{10}-1)/3)\lt19`$, so every integer
p at least 18 is excluded for exact complete sources. No width or
gate-count bound is used. A common source phase cancels from the
quadratic defect, so the same result holds after optimizing it.
This is a coarse complete-source screen; sharper obstructions under
the [specialized overlapping-source hypotheses](OVERLAPPING_TWO_BAND_SOURCES.md)
remain applicable.

### Approximate sources retain a shrinking threshold

Suppose instead that $`\|K-\alpha I_p\|\le\tau\alpha`$.
Put

```math
\eta=2\tau+\tau^2,\qquad
\beta_k=\frac{2^{k/2}}{\sqrt{B(M_k)}}.
```

Embed the ideal logical defect into the physical A code:

```math
X_*=\alpha^2 V_A(I_p^\dagger P I_p-P)V_A^\dagger.
```

The quadratic source estimate gives
$`\|X-X_*\|\le\alpha^2\eta`$. Both physical operators vanish
off the A code. Consequently

```math
\begin{aligned}
\|X\|&\ge\alpha^2(2-\eta),\\
\|X\|_{2,q+k}
&\le2^{-k/2}\alpha^2
             \left(\eta+2^{1-p/2}\right).
\end{aligned}
```

The second line uses the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm on all
logical inputs, followed by the exact clean-code factor
$`2^{-k/2}`$; it is not an initialized-subspace approximation to
the desired operator contract. If eta is at least two, the final
necessary bound below is automatic. Otherwise X is nonzero, and
combining these bounds with the Clifford rank--norm inequality
and cancelling positive alpha
gives the necessary condition

```math
\boxed{\quad
\eta\ge
\max\left\{0,
\frac{2(\beta_k-2^{-p/2})}{1+\beta_k}\right\}.
\quad}
```

Equivalently, whenever the fraction is positive,
$`\tau\ge\sqrt{1+2(\beta_k-2^{-p/2})/(1+\beta_k)}-1`$.
Setting tau to zero recovers the exact inequality above.

There is also a useful finite-error one-clean corollary: no complete
source with k equal to one, p at least 20 and positive alpha has
relative error at most $`1/1024`$. Indeed, put
$`R=\sqrt{B(10)/2}\lt420`$. Such an approximation would require
$`2-\eta\le R(\eta+2^{1-p/2})`$, while
$`\eta\lt1/500`$ and $`2^{1-p/2}\le1/512`$ give

```math
R(\eta+2^{1-p/2})\lt\frac{5313}{3200}
\lt\frac53\lt\frac{999}{500}\lt2-\eta.
```

This contradiction uses only the elementary elimination bound.
For fixed k this gives an explicit positive asymptotic relative-error
floor, uniform in amplitude and dirty width. For growing k, however,
$`\beta_k`$ decreases to zero. In particular this estimate does
not exclude fixed-accuracy approximate sources with
$`k=o(\log p)`$, including exactly sector-preserving sources
offered to the new promotion wrapper.

### A uniform precision tradeoff

Suppose the relative error is at most $`2^{-L}`$, where L is at
least two, and put $`s=\min\{L,p/2\}`$. Then
$`\eta\le3\cdot2^{-L}\le3/4`$, so the preceding necessary
inequality gives

```math
\frac54\le2-\eta
 \le R_k\bigl(\eta+2^{1-p/2}\bigr)
 \le5R_k2^{-s},\qquad
R_k=\sqrt{2^{-k}B(M_k)}.
```

Consequently $`\log_2R_k\ge\min\{L,p/2\}-2`$.
Substituting the optimized bound for B and inverting as above yields

```math
k\ge\frac23\log_2\min\{p,L\}
 -\frac13\log_2\log_2\min\{p,L\}-O(1)
```

as the minimum tends to infinity. This is a simultaneous
precision/clean-count constraint. For fixed L it gives no clean
count growing with p, so the constant-accuracy growing-clean
source question remains open.

All conclusions concern the complete compression of two actual
one-band encoders. They do not bound unrestricted native T-depth,
do not assume that the encoded target is Clifford, and do not close
the separate original physical-pair scout. The many-probe route
would still need a new uniform correlation theorem for its moving
one-band-encoded Clifford family to obtain a stronger growing-k
constant-accuracy conclusion.

## 11. A bounded-clean two-band screen for the normalized Gram interface

The rank--norm argument also applies directly to the
[paired Gram contract](NILPOTENT_PENCIL_OBSERVABLE_SOURCE.md#paired-projector-readout),
without assuming that the accepted block approximates a unitary. This
is a bounded source-screen corollary, not a general depth lower bound.
For each fixed clean count it gives only a **tiny-error** exclusion;
in particular it does not exclude every specified moderate tolerance.
The sharper
[one-clean two-band screen](NILPOTENT_PENCIL_OBSERVABLE_SOURCE.md#two-band-projector-gram-gap)
remains applicable under its own hypotheses.

Let $`J_k`$ insert k zero clean inputs, with k at least one, and let
$`U=U_B^\dagger U_A`$ be two actual one-band encoders. The q other
input wires, including address, banks and private work, are arbitrary.
Write

```math
K=J_k^\dagger UJ_k,\qquad G=K^\dagger K,\qquad
G_*={\Pi_{\rm eq}\over2}={I+\Omega_{x,C}\Omega_{x,D}\over4}.
```

Using B from Section 10, put

```math
M_k=2^{2k+1}(2^k-1),\qquad
\beta_k={2^{k/2}\over\sqrt{B(M_k)}}.
```

Any such source with $`\|G-G_*\|\le\delta`$ necessarily satisfies

```math
\boxed{\quad
\delta\ge {1\over4}\max\left\{0,
 {\beta_k-2^{-p/2}\over1+\beta_k}\right\}.
\quad}                                                        \qquad(11.1)
```

In particular an exact source requires
$`p+k\le\log_2 B(M_k)`$. For two clean inputs $`M_2=96`$.
For every fixed k, (11.1) has a positive limiting error floor as p
grows, but that floor shrinks rapidly with k. No fixed-accuracy
exclusion for growing k, or for a larger clean count chosen as the
requested accuracy changes, follows from this corollary alone.

To prove it, set $`V_A=U_AJ_k`$,
$`P_A=V_AV_A^\dagger`$ and
$`P_B=U_BJ_kJ_k^\dagger U_B^\dagger`$. Choose one bank bit at
row u in C and let L be its X Pauli, acting trivially on the clean
inputs. Its one-band image $`D=U_ALU_A^\dagger`$ is a literal
Clifford and commutes with $`P_A`$. Hence the physical defect is

```math
E=P_A(P_B-DP_BD^\dagger)P_A
  =V_A(G-LGL)V_A^\dagger.                                    \qquad(11.2)
```

On the right L denotes the same Pauli on the logical input. Each
code projector is $`2^{-k}`$ times the sum of its $`2^k`$
one-band images of clean-Z products; all these images are literal
Cliffords. The identity term cancels in the middle difference of
(11.2). Expanding both outer projectors and both middle terms
therefore gives at most $`2\cdot2^{2k}(2^k-1)=M_k`$ Clifford
summands. No collapse of ordered products or flatness is assumed.

The desired full-bank action has the exact one-address defect

```math
G_*-LG_*L={1\over2}[x=u]\Omega_{x,C}\Omega_{x,D}.
```

For its encoded version $`E_*`$, including all logical bank and
private-work columns and using physical normalized trace,

```math
\|E_*\|={1\over2},\qquad
\|E_*\|_{2,q+k}={1\over2}2^{-(p+k)/2}.
```

The Gram error gives $`\|E-E_*\|\le2\delta`$. Both defects are
supported on $`P_A`$, so their normalized Hilbert--Schmidt error is
at most $`2^{-k/2}2\delta`$, rather than merely $`2\delta`$.
For $`\delta\lt1/4`$, the rank--norm inequality now implies

```math
{1\over2}-2\delta
 \le\sqrt{B(M_k)}\,2^{-k/2}
       \left(2\delta+{1\over2}2^{-p/2}\right),
\qquad
\beta_k(1-4\delta)\le4\delta+2^{-p/2},
```

which rearranges to (11.1). For $`\delta\ge1/4`$ that bound is
automatic. Intermediate address motion, arbitrary dirty width and
arbitrary reference entanglement do not affect this proof; no
complete-return promise or count budget was used. Thus merely adding
a second clean input does not give a two-band source meeting this
Gram contract at every requested accuracy. Genuine higher-band
interference remains outside this screen.

encoded defect and its displayed expansion over
$`\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,i)`$ for the single-bank Clifford-conjugate target.
A separate exact fixture checks the canonical paired lookup support
and physical trace factors. These finite fixtures are not a proof of
