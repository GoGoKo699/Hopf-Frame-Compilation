# Global constraints for a quadratically controlled Pauli insertion

[Actual clean-transition form](COMPUTATIONAL_PHASE_DERIVATIVES.md#10-the-actual-clean-transition-leaves-a-quadratic-controlled-pauli) ·
[Finite insertion](FINITE_INSERTION_THREE_LAYER_FEATURES.md) ·
[Representation parameters](CLEAN_TRANSITION_PARAMETERS.md)

A quadratic control of bounded polar rank reduces to the retained finite
insertion theorem using actual Clifford operations. At arbitrary nonzero
polar rank, the associated Clifford probe images preserve the middle native
span and agree on a common invariant isotropic algebra. Enlarging the actual shared
center makes its endpoint images monomial Cliffords. These statements
retain the original independent native lists; a global decoder or a bound
on the remaining relative center phases is still missing.

The [constant-translation continuation](CONSTANT_TRANSLATION_NATIVE_CLOSURE.md)
closes that monomial subcase with sign/XOR bounds three/two. The
[center and decoder audit](NATIVE_CENTER_SATURATION_LIMITS.md) strengthens
the genuine center but shows why a fixed Clifford decoder is not a
general shortcut. Center-dependent phase matching remains open.

## 1. Actual input controls and the full-space word

Use the no-middle-pair clean-transition reduction with both interior
clean images non-Pauli. After its paid real quadratic normalization and
fixed Clifford changes, retain the central cubic sign separately. The
remaining full logical operator, including all arbitrary dirty inputs,
is Boolean diagonal and has the literal form

```math
V=K_C^\dagger E_C^\dagger F E_BE_AK_A
  =e^{i\theta}D_g,
\qquad F=H^{b(z)}.                                      \qquad(1)
```

All three E lists are independent native T/T-dagger bands. The signed
Hermitian Pauli H commutes with a shared computational Z subgroup R,
whose genuine input eigenbits are z, and with every middle-axis Pauli.
Every band and both normalized endpoints fix R pointwise. The Boolean
polynomial b has degree at most two. The literal source of F is the
retained clean-transition product and its aligning Clifford, not a free
controlled gate. Its square is I. The central cubic and earlier real
quadratic signs are part of the exact target factorization.

The arguments below also apply to any independently supplied full-space
word satisfying these premises. They do not authorize a separate circuit
extension on each fixed z fiber.

For a quadratic Boolean b, define its alternating polar form by

```math
\beta_b(u,v)=b(u+v)+b(u)+b(v)+b(0).
```

Its binary rank is even; write it as $`2r`$. This rank ignores the
affine part of b and is invariant under invertible affine changes of its
actual center coordinates.

## 2. Fixed polar rank gives a uniform degree bound

A linear symplectic basis for the polar form gives actual center parities
$`u_1(z),\ldots,u_{2r}(z)`$ and an affine Boolean function ell such that

```math
b(z)=\ell(z)+q(z),\qquad
q(z)=\sum_{j=1}^r u_{2j-1}(z)u_{2j}(z).             \qquad(2)
```

The operation $`C_\ell=H^{\ell(z)}`$ is an actual Clifford, including
its literal sign. Indeed a constant control contributes either I or the
signed Pauli H. A linear control is the controlled-H operation selected
by one signed center parity. For two independent commuting Pauli labels,
a fixed Clifford coordinate word turns this into CZ; coincident or
identity labels reduce to a Pauli or scalar. The defining projector
formula retains those special cases without a phase ambiguity. This
uses $`O(Q^2)`$ elementary Clifford gates on Q logical wires, no T gates
and no initialized helper. Its actual inverse is the reversed inverse
word, with its own corresponding ledger.

Since H and every center parity commute each middle axis, C_ell does too.
Put

```math
F_q=H^{q(z)},\qquad
E_C'=C_\ell^\dagger E_CC_\ell,\qquad
K_C'=C_\ell^\dagger K_C.
```

The exact full-space identity is

```math
V=(K_C')^\dagger(E_C')^\dagger F_qE_BE_AK_A.       \qquad(3)
```

The output list has only been conjugated by a fixed actual Clifford;
its independence, size and orientations are unchanged. The input and
middle lists are unchanged. No conditional coordinate choices or
unpaid higher-level corrections enter (3).

F_q belongs to the commuting Pauli algebra generated by H and the
$`2r`$ actual center parities in (2). Thus its support-space dimension
is at most $`2r+1`$, independently of the number of remaining center
variables, dirty width or endpoint clean-active ranks. It still commutes
every middle-axis Pauli. The retained finite-insertion theorem proves

```math
\deg g\le D_{\mathrm{ins}}(0,0,0,2r+1)
\quad\text{for }r\ge1.                              \qquad(4)
```

The explicit function D_ins is defined in
[its proof home](FINITE_INSERTION_THREE_LAYER_FEATURES.md#1-the-full-space-expression-and-explicit-bound).
When r=0 the entire insertion is Clifford and is absorbed in (3),
leaving an actual zero-clean three-native-band Boolean word. The sharper
bound is then $`\deg g\le5`$. Restoring the retained central cubic and
quadratic signs does not raise either bound. The fixed coordinate changes
preserve Boolean degree; the previously used Hadamards acted only on
exact identity modes.

For an XOR query, first pass to its actual output-Hadamard sign
representation and check these hypotheses there. For a nonzero predicate,
its degree is one less than the corresponding sign bound. This is a
component uniform in growing endpoint ranks when polar rank stays fixed,
not a universal bound on polar rank or an unchanged-cost synthesis of
the original controlled insertion. The retained exact-rounding floor
applies first when an approximate occupied Boolean query is brought
below that floor.

## 3. Full affine control support forces Clifford rigidity

Let C be an actual full-space Clifford fixing the computational center
R pointwise. Let S be a nonempty set of its center eigenvalues. Suppose
C is computationally diagonal on the **entire** remaining quantum fiber
for every z in S. If S affinely spans the center-coordinate space, then
C is computationally diagonal on the full register.

For each computational Z_i, the operator $`P_i=CZ_iC^\dagger`$ is
an actual signed Pauli. On every full fiber z in S it agrees with Z_i.
A Pauli agreeing there cannot translate any free quantum coordinate,
and cannot have a nontrivial Z character on those arbitrary coordinates.
Consequently

```math
P_iZ_i=(-1)^{\ell_i(z)}
```

is a signed center character, with affine ell_i vanishing on S. Full
affine span makes each ell_i identically zero. C therefore fixes every
computational Z_i literally, which proves global diagonality. This is a
full-operator Pauli argument, not an occupied Clifford extension.

### 3.1. Quadratic controls of polar rank at least four have full affine support

Take $`S=\{z:b(z)=1\}`$. If this support is contained in a proper
affine hyperplane, choose an affine coordinate z_1 whose complementary
hyperplane contains the support. The unique Boolean ANF then has the
form

```math
b(z)=(1+z_1)\,a(z_2,\ldots),\qquad \deg a\le1.     \qquad(5)
```

Its polar rank is at most two. Hence polar rank at least four implies
full affine span, and the preceding rigidity theorem applies. The exact
condition is affine span; some controls of smaller polar rank also have
it. A nonzero quadratic control failing that condition factors into at
most two affine functions. The zero control is treated separately.

### 3.2. A matching native-band sandwich cannot hide a high-rank control

Let E be one actual independent native phase band centralizing R, and
let H be a signed Hermitian Pauli commuting with R. Suppose

```math
E^\dagger H^{b(z)}E
```

is computationally diagonal on the full register. On every b=1 full
fiber the actual Hermitian Clifford $`C=E^\dagger HE`$ is diagonal.
If the active support of b has full affine span, Section 3 makes C
globally diagonal.

The intrinsic displacement image of a native one-band Pauli image is
exactly the span of its activated independent axes. Since C is diagonal,
that image lies in the computational Z space. Thus every E axis active
against H is itself computational Z. The exact one-band formula writes
C as H times diagonal S rotations on those axes, with its literal scalar.
Those rotations cannot change H's computational X mask. Diagonality of
C therefore makes H a computational Z Pauli, which cannot activate any
of those Z axes. The active sublist is empty:

```math
[E,H]=0,\qquad H\text{ is computational Z}.         \qquad(6)
```

In particular this holds whenever the polar rank of b is at least four.
It requires no one-core restriction on E. At polar rank two the
conclusion can fail. On a core t and center bits z_1,z_2, take

```math
E=T_{X_tZ_1}T_{X_tZ_2},\qquad H=Z_t,
\qquad b=z_1(1+z_2).
```

The two native axes are independent. On the only active center fiber
$`(z_1,z_2)=(1,0)`$, E is literally $`\omega I`$, so
$`E^\dagger H^bE=H^b`$ is diagonal. Yet E does not commute with H
on the whole register. This checks the support requirement and retained
scalar, rather than providing a higher-degree target.

For the actual word (1), no theorem in this section identifies its two distinct
endpoint bands with one E sandwich. Conjugating through the middle
band can leave a Clifford with growing displacement. Establishing that
same-band diagonal condition, or a replacement global matching identity,
is not supplied by this sandwich argument. Section 5 obtains a global
invariant span directly from (1), without asserting a same-band sandwich.

## 4. An actual easy source has unbounded control polar rank

For every positive even integer m, use one clean c, a logical h, and
m logical pairs $`x_j,y_j`$. Choose $`t_j\in\{1,-1\}`$ with
$`\sum_jt_j=0`$. Write c+x for the positive Z Pauli $`Z_cZ_x`$,
and similarly for longer sums. Set

```math
\begin{aligned}
E_A&=\prod_j T_{c+x_j}^{t_j}T_{c+h+y_j}^{-t_j},\\
E_B&=\prod_j T_{c+y_j}^{t_j}T_{c+x_j+y_j}^{-t_j},\\
E_C&=\prod_j T_{c+h+x_j}^{t_j}T_{c+h+x_j+y_j}^{-t_j},\\
K_A&=K_C=H_c.
\end{aligned}                                                       \qquad(7)
```

Each band has 2m independent axes. Its projections onto the local
$`(x_j,y_j)`$ coordinates are respectively the independent pairs
$`\{10,01\}`$, $`\{01,11\}`$, and $`\{10,11\}`$. Different blocks
have disjoint such coordinates, so no within-band relation occurs.
All axes commute and are clean-odd for $`P_A=P_C=X_c`$.
Both interior clean ranks are 2m; every middle orbit is a negative
singleton, so there are no middle pairs.

The eight-parity CCZ identity, with the two common axes c and c+h
canceled by the exact sum of the t_j, gives

```math
F_0=E_C^\dagger E_BE_A
 =I_c\otimes(-1)^{h\sum_jx_jy_j}.                  \qquad(8)
```

This identity holds on every physical column, including the unused
clean sector, with no omitted scalar. Thus the aligning Clifford can
be I. The common active span has rank $`2m+2`$. Its endpoint
intersection is clean-even, and the active-difference center is

```math
R=\mathrm{span}\{Z_{y_j},Z_hZ_{x_j}:1\le j\le m\}.
```

Let its actual eigenbits be y_j and $`z_j=h+x_j`$, and choose the
extra Pauli $`H=Z_h`$, the literal product of same-x A/C axes. Then

```math
F_0=H^{b(z,y)},\qquad
b(z,y)=\sum_j\bigl(z_jy_j+y_j\bigr),\qquad
\mathrm{rank}(\beta_b)=2m.                         \qquad(9)
```

The target degree is only three. Dropping Z_c from every phase axis
also leaves three independent logical lists implementing that same sign.
This is an equally shallow zero-clean native baseline.

For explicit elementary coordinate words, a band is obtained by the
inverse of its following Clifford change, its T/T-dagger rotations on
the indicated local wires, then the change. For each block use

| Band | Coordinate change in chronological order | Phase targets and orientations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | CNOT c to x; CNOT c to y; CNOT h to y | x with t; y with minus t |
| B | CNOT y to x; CNOT c to y | y with t; x with minus t |
| C | CNOT x to y; CNOT c to x; CNOT h to x | x with t; y with minus t |

These changes fix the shared controls, so the block words combine
without extra cross terms. The displayed full one-clean word and its
actual reversed inverse each have T-count 6m, native T-depth at most
three, and $`16m+2`$ elementary Cliffords. Removing the c gates and
endpoint Hadamards gives the zero-clean baseline and its inverse with
6m T gates, depth at most three, and 8m Cliffords. All logical and any
additional untouched dirty inputs are arbitrary, including references.

**Scope of the witness.** Every band in (7) commutes Z_c. Thus Z_c is
a shared clean flip, and the already proved five/four criterion closes
this family. It does not show unbounded polar rank in the remaining
branch without a shared clean flip. Equation (9) is a parameter of this
retained source and aligner, not the minimum over every occupied-equivalent
representation or unused-sector extension. It prevents using a universal
raw polar-rank bound as a consequence of actual clean origin alone.

The simplest attempt to break the shared flip by appending a literally
canceling silent T pair does not work for this family. Every nonzero
logical translation of $`h\sum_jx_jy_j`$ has a nonzero quadratic
part in its derivative. Hence F_0 transports a Pauli to a Pauli only
when that Pauli has zero logical X mask. If it is also A-silent,
commutation with the A-active axes forces its clean X mask to vanish.
Such a pair cannot remove the shared Z_c flip. This is a constraint on
that proposed extension, not an exclusion of more general native words.

The [exact fixtures](../../tests/test_quadratic_control_native_matching.py)
check the literal affine-controlled Clifford absorption and output-band
conjugation, the rank-two boundary example, and the actual all-column
family for m=2 and m=4. The arbitrary-width rank statement and all
resource conclusions are proved above, rather than inferred from those
finite checks. No complete-frame resource frontier is changed.

## 5. A genuine quadratic control forces a common invariant middle span

Return to the exact full-space word (1), and suppose b has nonzero
polar rank. For every computational probe label u define the actual Hermitian
Cliffords

```math
J_u=E_AK_AZ(u)K_A^\dagger E_A^\dagger,\qquad
C_u=E_CK_CZ(u)K_C^\dagger E_C^\dagger.
```

The diagonal target gives the literal matching identity

```math
C_u=F E_BJ_uE_B^\dagger F.                         \qquad(10)
```

Both families fix R pointwise: every endpoint and band fixes R, and
the computational probes commute with it. Write L_B for the span of
the original independent middle axes. Then, simultaneously for every probe u,

```math
J_uL_B=L_B,\qquad
J_uHJ_u^\dagger\,H\in\mathrm{Alg}(R+L_B).          \qquad(11)
```

In the second statement the displayed operator is a signed Hermitian
Pauli. The common isotropic space $`R+L_B+\langle h\rangle`$ is
invariant under both Clifford families, and their literal conjugation
actions agree on its entire Pauli algebra. This is span preservation,
not a permutation theorem for the individual native axes.

### 5.1. Every middle-axis probe has trivial native activation

Let V be a signed Hermitian middle-axis Pauli, and put

```math
P=J_uVJ_u^\dagger,\qquad X=E_BPE_B^\dagger,
\qquad D_b=(-1)^{b(z)}.
```

Here P commutes R, and X is an actual one-band Hermitian Clifford.
Every Pauli term in X differs from P by a product of middle axes.
Those axes commute H, so all terms have the same H-pairing e as P.
They also commute R. Consequently

```math
FXF=D_b^eX=C_uVC_u^\dagger.                       \qquad(12)
```

The right side is Pauli. If e=1, X is D_b times a Pauli, so its
Clifford displacement image lies in R. Native middle-axis independence
identifies that image with the span of all axes activated by P.
Every activated axis would therefore lie in R, contradicting the fact
that P commutes R. The active set is empty, making X Pauli and then
forcing D_b Pauli. The latter is impossible because b has a genuine
quadratic part. Thus e=0. Equation (12) and the same independence again
force the active set to be empty. Literally,

```math
C_uVC_u^\dagger=J_uVJ_u^\dagger,
\qquad J_uVJ_u^\dagger\text{ commutes }R,L_B,H.    \qquad(13)
```

The proof uses the original physical native list on the full logical
register, including all arbitrary dirty inputs. No center fiber was
selected and no projected axis relation was discarded.

### 5.2. Squaring the commuting defect recovers the whole middle span

For a fixed probe u set

```math
W_u=R+L_B+J_uL_B,\qquad H_u=J_uHJ_u^\dagger,
\qquad D_u=E_BJ_uE_B^\dagger J_u^\dagger.
```

By (13), W_u is isotropic and commutes with H. It also commutes with
H_u: this is immediate on R and J_uL_B, and on L_B it follows from
(13) and $`J_u^2=I`$. D_u is the literal commuting phase product
on the two lists B and J_uB, with every sign and orientation retained.
Since J_u fixes the controls pointwise, (10) becomes

```math
K_u:=C_uJ_u^\dagger=D_uH^{b(z)}H_u^{b(z)}.         \qquad(14)
```

For any Pauli Q in $`W_u^\perp`$, conjugation by this actual Clifford
multiplies Q by

```math
(-1)^{(\langle q,h\rangle+\langle q,h_u\rangle)b(z)}.
```

A Clifford image must be Pauli, whereas D_b is not Pauli. Therefore
the displayed pairing sum vanishes for every such Q. Symplectic
duality gives $`h+h_u\in W_u`$. Since H commutes W_u, H and H_u
commute, and their literal product $`P_u=HH_u`$ is a signed Hermitian
Pauli in that space. Thus

```math
K_u=D_uP_u^{b(z)}\in\mathrm{Alg}(W_u).
```

A Clifford in an isotropic Pauli algebra becomes a diagonal Clifford
in fixed Clifford coordinates; its square is a Pauli unitary, including
its scalar. D_u and the Boolean factor commute, so
$`D_u^2=K_u^2`$ is also a Pauli unitary. Squaring the two native
phase lists gives the binary Gram identity

```math
\sum_{v\in B}vv^{\mathsf T}
 =\sum_{v\in B}(J_uv)(J_uv)^{\mathsf T}.          \qquad(15)
```

For an independent list, its Gram matrix has image equal to its span.
Both lists in (15) are independent. Hence $`J_uL_B=L_B`$, proving
(11), and W_u is the same space $`R+L_B`$ for every u. Equations
(13)--(14) now show that C_u and J_u agree literally on R, L_B and H,
and preserve their common isotropic span.

All steps are identities of the retained full-space word and actual
Clifford Pauli actions. No replacement circuit or new gate synthesis
is introduced. Different independent bases can have the same Gram
matrix and leave a Boolean cubic phase, as the retained
[exact Gram fixture](../../tests/test_computational_phase_derivatives.py)
demonstrates. Thus (15) does not justify replacing the native middle
list by an unpaid new basis, or declaring the endpoint encoding of
this invariant algebra to be Clifford. Further global matching is
still required for an unrestricted degree conclusion.

### 5.3. A larger actual center gives monomial Clifford endpoint images

There is a further simultaneous consequence of the whole probe family.
Put

```math
S=R+L_B+\langle h\rangle,\qquad
R_0=\mathrm{span}\{(\Phi_{J_u}-I)s:u\text{ arbitrary},\ s\in S\},
```

where Phi denotes the binary symplectic action. The native one-band
formula gives
$`\mathrm{im}(\Phi_{J_u}-I)\subseteq L_A`$ and
$`\mathrm{im}(\Phi_{C_u}-I)\subseteq L_C`$.
Section 5.2 shows that the two actions agree on S and preserve S.
Consequently

```math
R_0\subseteq S\cap L_A\cap L_C.
```

The space S is isotropic. Thus R_0 commutes with R, H and every middle
axis; its membership in both endpoint spans makes it commute with
every endpoint axis as well. The enlarged space
$`\widetilde R=R+R_0`$ is therefore an actual shared center of all
retained factors. This conclusion concerns the original global axes,
not their restrictions to selected center eigenvalues.

Apply the
[actual shared-center normalization](COMPUTATIONAL_PHASE_DERIVATIVES.md#7-boolean-reality-gives-a-shared-computational-center)
to this enlarged center. Its Pauli intertwinings have affine Boolean
derivatives, so the aligning input phase is an actual real quadratic
Clifford. Retain that phase in the target. The remaining affine
coordinate changes, diagonal Clifford decorations and Hadamards on
exact identity modes make the center computational simultaneously at
both endpoints. These are fixed Clifford words, with
$`O(Q^2)`$ elementary Clifford cost and separately charged actual
inverse words. The old center can be retained while choosing coordinates
on its complement. No conditional basis choice, initialized helper or
new T band is introduced, and native list independence is preserved.

In these normalized coordinates write z for the enlarged center and
y for all remaining computational coordinates, including arbitrary
dirty inputs. For a signed Hermitian Pauli P_s with label s in S,
consider its two endpoint images

```math
Q_A(s)=K_A^\dagger E_A^\dagger P_sE_AK_A,\qquad
Q_C(s)=K_C^\dagger E_C^\dagger P_sE_CK_C.
```

They are actual Hermitian Cliffords. Each family is commuting, and
each member fixes the enlarged center pointwise. They are also
**monomial Cliffords**: their computational action has the form

```math
Q_A(s)|z,y\rangle
 =\gamma_s i^{\ell_s(z,y)}(-1)^{q_s(z,y)}
   |z,y+a_s+L_sz\rangle.
```

Here a_s is fixed, L_s is binary linear, ell_s is an integer-linear
phase modulo four, q_s is Boolean quadratic, and gamma_s is the
retained literal constant phase. The analogous expression for Q_C(s)
has the same permutation of computational basis states. Quadratic
phases in the work coordinates are allowed.

To verify monomiality without changing the probe hypothesis, first use
the original computational probes. The commutator
$`Q_A(s)Z(u)Q_A(s)^\dagger Z(u)`$ is the signed Pauli with label
$`(\Phi_{J_u}-I)s`$, pulled back through the input encoder. The
intervening E_A fixes this Pauli because its label lies in R_0. It is
therefore a center Pauli. The same assertion holds at the output.
After normalization, the new computational Z group is generated by
retained affine combinations of these old Z probes together with
the newly computational center generators from the exact identity
modes. The endpoint images fix the latter generators literally.
Their action on every new computational Z hence differs from that Z
only by a signed center Pauli. This proves both preservation of the
computational Z group and the displayed affine center-controlled
translation, with no work-to-work linear part. Removing that actual
affine Clifford permutation leaves a diagonal Clifford, giving the
stated phase form. Finally, the middle operation lies in Alg(S), so

```math
V^\dagger Q_C(s)V=Q_A(s)
```

holds literally. Since the normalized target remains Boolean diagonal,
it cannot change the basis permutation, proving that the two endpoint
images have the same translation.

This identifies an actual commuting monomial Clifford algebra; it does
not identify it with a Pauli algebra or provide a Clifford decoder for
it. The identities do not bound the number of remaining work modes or
the rank of their center-controlled translation matrices. A decoder
chosen by separate Gaussian elimination for each z, or a deletion of
the quadratic work phases, is not supplied by these identities and
would require an actual global synthesis and its resource account.
The enlarged center and monomial form therefore refine the next
matching question without establishing an unrestricted phase-degree
bound.

## 6. What central transport does not determine

### 6.1. The recorded equations leave relative center phases undetermined

Let T be the normalized computational parity space in
[the endpoint-span derivative theorem](COMPUTATIONAL_PHASE_DERIVATIVES.md#6-a-broader-derivative-theorem-for-a-commuting-c3-sandwich).
It consists of designated-input Z parities with identity on every dirty
wire. Choose a basis of T and let pi_T send an input x to those parity
eigenbits. Its kernel is the corresponding derivative space
$`U=T^\perp`$. For an arbitrary Boolean function eta of these eigenbits,
put

```math
g_\eta(x)=g(x)+\eta(\pi_Tx).
```

Then, literally,

```math
\Delta_u g_\eta=\Delta_u g\qquad(u\in U).
```

The recorded shared-center commutators and common-lift intertwinings
are also unchanged. This is an invariance of those operator data,
including their signs, not a new native implementation of g_eta.

In the actual clean-derived case, the limitation can also be seen on
the whole physical register. Let V_phys denote the underlying actual
clean-preserving unitary in normalized clean coordinates. Write D_eta
for the diagonal sign selected by eta and form

```math
V_{\mathrm{phys},\eta}=V_{\mathrm{phys}}(I_c\otimes D_\eta).
```

This full unitary has the same computational-Z probe images and exact
clean return as V_phys, while its occupied sign changes by D_eta. No scalar
has been discarded. But D_eta generally cannot be absorbed into an
actual Clifford endpoint, and the modified unitary need not have the original
three-native-band realization. Thus the invariance is a limitation
of the retained transport equations, not a high-degree native family
or an obstruction for the unrestricted model.

For a direction u outside U, choose t in T with $`u\cdot t=1`$.
Every Pauli with designated X mask u anticommutes with Z(t), regardless
of its Z dressing or its dirty-register Pauli factor. Since Z(t)
belongs to both transformed endpoint spans, that Pauli activates at
least one axis in each endpoint list. Neither endpoint-commutation
cancellation in the cited derivative theorem therefore supplies its
two-band derivative. This does not exclude other cancellations, such
as allowing activated endpoint axes that individually commute with
the middle operation. A new global argument must constrain relative
center phases while retaining the actual native lists. Removing the
formal quartic part or fixing center fibers does not supply that argument.

### 6.2. Sliding the insertion preserves relations but changes the axes

There is an exact way to identify the unpaid operation when attempting
to move F through an endpoint band. Every native axis A_i commutes the
actual control center, so

```math
F A_i F
 =A_iD_b^{\langle a_i,h\rangle},
\qquad D_b=(-1)^{b(z)}.                              \qquad(16)
```

Conjugation by the same F preserves commutativity, Hermiticity and the
absence of multiplicative relations among these involutions. It also
gives the literal identity
$`F T_{A_i}^{\sigma_i} F=T_{F A_i F}^{\sigma_i}`$,
with the original signed axis, orientation and scalar retained.

If b has a genuine quadratic part, D_b is a non-Pauli Clifford. Every
axis active for H in (16) is then a non-Pauli Clifford, even though
the transformed family is still independent as a family of commuting
involutions. Its spectral T rotations are not the physical Pauli-axis
native band required by the model. Crediting that family as a native
band would therefore leave the first operation unpaid. An alternative
actual native synthesis could still exist; (16) neither constructs it
nor excludes it. When b is affine, F is an actual Clifford and the
paid absorption of Section 2 already applies.
