# Native center saturation and the limit of Clifford decoding

[Quadratic-control matching](QUADRATIC_CONTROL_NATIVE_MATCHING.md) ·
[Computational phase derivatives](COMPUTATIONAL_PHASE_DERIVATIVES.md) ·
[Actual native witness](ACTUAL_NATIVE_CENTER_WITNESS.md#4-the-surviving-center-and-encoded-algebra)

The native endpoint encoders supply further genuine shared centers.
Their active-axis sums and the whole-word Galois products retain
information that is absent from an arbitrary commuting monomial
Clifford group. These enlargements do not, however, force constant
work translations. A fixed Clifford decoder has an exact stronger
limitation: it can turn a one-native-band image of an isotropic Pauli
algebra into a Pauli algebra only when that native band already
centralizes the original algebra.

These are statements about retained native representations. They do
not exclude a different non-Clifford mechanism, an occupied-equivalent
extension, or a uniform Boolean-degree theorem. The actual witness
below has degree three.

## 1. Full-space hypotheses and the invariant algebra

Use the retained full-space logical word

```math
V=K_C^\dagger E_C^\dagger F E_BE_AK_A
  =e^{i\theta}D_g,\qquad F=H^{b(z)}.
```

The three E lists are independent commuting signed Pauli T/T-dagger
bands. Every band and both normalized endpoints fix the computational
center R pointwise. H is a signed Hermitian Pauli commuting with R and
with every middle axis. The control b is Boolean quadratic with nonzero
polar rank. All logical and dirty inputs are arbitrary; every equality
is on the full logical space, including references. The literal central
cubic and quadratic signs removed in reaching this word remain in the
target degree account.

For each computational Z probe define

```math
J_u=E_AK_AZ(u)K_A^\dagger E_A^\dagger,\qquad
C_u=E_CK_CZ(u)K_C^\dagger E_C^\dagger.
```

Let L_A, L_B and L_C be the native axis spans, and put

```math
S=R+L_B+\langle h\rangle.
```

The [whole-family matching theorem](QUADRATIC_CONTROL_NATIVE_MATCHING.md#5-a-genuine-quadratic-control-forces-a-common-invariant-middle-span)
makes S isotropic and invariant under J_u and C_u, with identical
literal actions there. Its displacement enlargement is

```math
R_0=\mathrm{span}\{(\Phi_{J_u}-I)s:u\text{ arbitrary},\ s\in S\}
 \subseteq S\cap L_A\cap L_C.
```

The [paid normalization](QUADRATIC_CONTROL_NATIVE_MATCHING.md#53-a-larger-actual-center-gives-monomial-clifford-endpoint-images)
makes R+R_0 a genuine computational center. For every signed Pauli P_s
in S, the endpoint images

```math
Q_A(s)=K_A^\dagger E_A^\dagger P_sE_AK_A,\qquad
Q_C(s)=K_C^\dagger E_C^\dagger P_sE_CK_C
```

are commuting Hermitian monomial Clifford families satisfying
$`V^\dagger Q_C(s)V=Q_A(s)`$. Their common basis permutation has
the form

```math
(z,y)\longmapsto(z,y+a_s+L_sz).
```

Their diagonal Clifford phases, including quadratic work phases and
literal fourth- or eighth-root constants, have not been discarded.

Section 3 additionally uses the remaining complementary branch: every
computational parity in the common endpoint space

```math
T=M\cap K_A^\dagger L_AK_A\cap K_C^\dagger L_CK_C
```

has a literal shared middle lift commuting with every retained factor.
This is the [proved common-lift conclusion](COMPUTATIONAL_PHASE_DERIVATIVES.md#95-the-complementary-common-parities-have-literal-shared-lifts)
for the actual clean-derived branch still under consideration. With the
independent logical middle list used here, it also follows directly
when every input lift of T commutes H: the diagonal target reduces its
middle transport to a one-band Pauli image, whose activated native
sublist must be empty. The other sections state their own hypotheses
and do not assume a separate center-fiber extension.

## 2. Repeating the same displacement enlargement adds no center

For the fixed S and fixed native encoders above, repeating the R_0
construction after its prescribed normalization adds no new direction
outside the already enlarged center. This is a scope-specific
idempotence statement, not a claim about every possible resplitting.

The real quadratic input Clifford used in the alignment commutes with
every old computational Z probe. The affine coordinate changes replace
these probes by linear combinations. The remaining Hadamards act only
on exact identity modes of the residual target. Its new computational
Z group is therefore generated by retained old-Z combinations and by
the newly computational shared-center Paulis on those identity modes.

An old-Z combination moves S only by R_0, by its definition. An added
center generator pulls through the endpoint and native band as its
actual middle Pauli, which centralizes S. Its displacement of S is
zero. Thus the new displacement span is contained in the center
already adjoined. This argument tracks the changed probes directly;
it does not assume that the old all-probe theorem automatically applies
after a Hadamard change.

The real quadratic sign, affine input change and identity-mode
Hadamards keep their exact target interpretation. Their fixed Clifford
words and actual inverse words have the existing
$`O(Q^2)`$ Clifford account. Iteration supplies neither a new T band
nor a reason for the matrices L_s to vanish.

## 3. Native characteristic sums give additional genuine centers

For each s in S define the intrinsic endpoint sums

```math
w_A(s)=\sum_{a\in A:\langle a,s\rangle=1}a,\qquad
w_C(s)=\sum_{c\in C:\langle c,s\rangle=1}c.
```

In the complementary branch of Section 1, every such label has a
literal shared lift commuting with all retained factors. Consequently

```math
W_A=\mathrm{span}\{w_A(s):s\in S\},\qquad
W_C=\mathrm{span}\{w_C(s):s\in S\}
```

may be adjoined to the shared center. These spaces need not be contained
in the earlier displacement center R_0.

### 3.1. Keep the literal scalar in the conjugate product

Write an overline for entrywise complex conjugation in the computational
basis. If Q is a Hermitian monomial Clifford, then
$`Q\overline Q`$ is a Pauli unitary with a computational Z label.
Its scalar need not be real. For example,

```math
Q=(X+Y)/\sqrt2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Q\overline Q=-iZ.
```

Indeed Q and its conjugate have the same involutory basis permutation,
so their product is diagonal. The product is Pauli because a Hermitian
Clifford and its conjugate have the same involutory binary symplectic
action. Its scalar is retained as a fourth root.

The real diagonal sign D_g intertwines Q_C(s) and Q_A(s). Therefore

```math
Q_A(s)\overline{Q_A(s)}
 =D_g Q_C(s)\overline{Q_C(s)}D_g
 =Q_C(s)\overline{Q_C(s)}.
```

This is an equality of literal Pauli unitaries, not just their labels.

### 3.2. The native label formula and a diagonal proof gauge

For a Hermitian Pauli label v let
$`\kappa(v)=x(v)\cdot z(v)`$ be its Y parity. The independent native
one-band image of a Pauli has binary displacement

```math
N(v)=\sum_{a\text{ active}}\langle a,v\rangle a.
```

Since the activated axes commute, its conjugate-product label is

```math
k=\sum_{a\text{ active}}(1+\kappa(a))a.             \qquad(1)
```

One way to check (1) is to use the defining Pauli action of complex
conjugation. Its label is characterized by
$`\langle k,v\rangle=\kappa((I+N)v)+\kappa(v)`$.
The quadratic polarization identity and isotropy of the active axis
span give precisely the displayed sum. Native orientations and signed
axis representatives do not alter this binary formula; their literal
scalar remains in the preceding operator identity.

Now apply a fixed diagonal S-word to the input coordinates that makes
every pulled-back A axis real. Such a word always exists. On an
isotropic axis space, kappa is linear and vanishes on the kernel of
the X projection. It therefore factors through that X projection and
extends to a linear functional on all X masks. S gates on the selected
coordinates toggle exactly that functional.

Conjugate both endpoint image families by this same diagonal Clifford.
It commutes with D_g, fixes every computational Z parity, and preserves
monomiality. Formula (1) now makes the A-side conjugate-product label
the sum of all active A axes. The identical C-side label is some
combination of C axes. Pulling the proof gauge back shows that
$`K_A^\dagger w_A(s)K_A`$ is a computational Z parity in T.
The common-lift hypothesis makes its physical middle lift genuinely
shared. Choosing a separate diagonal proof gauge for the C list gives
the same conclusion for w_C(s). Both conclusions are pulled back to
the original frame; no incompatible simultaneous gauge is assumed.

The resulting centers lie in both endpoint spans, centralize H and
every B axis, and hence commute with S. After adjoining W_A+W_C,
the same characteristic construction produces nothing further: every
newly adjoined direction activates neither endpoint list. Thus this
is a stronger but still finite saturation for the fixed S and encoders.
Its proof does not replace the native lists by arbitrary orthogonal
bases or declare their center-fiber restrictions independent.

## 4. Whole-word Galois products supply at most two further directions

There is a complementary consequence of the native automorphism
already used in the
[two-layer rational-return theorem](UNRESTRICTED_TWO_LAYER_QUERY.md#5-exact-rational-return-has-a-native-galois-constraint).
Let $`\mathsf P_A,\mathsf P_B,\mathsf P_C`$ be the literal products
of all signed Hermitian native axes in their respective lists, each
included once regardless of its T/T-dagger orientation. An empty list
contributes I. These symbols are not the earlier physical clean Paulis.

All three products are shared by every retained factor, and

```math
\mathsf P_A=\epsilon\mathsf P_B\mathsf P_C,
\qquad\epsilon\in\{1,-1\}.                       \qquad(2)
```

They span an isotropic shared label space of dimension at most two.
They need not belong to W_A+W_C: an all-axes activation pattern need
not be attained by a probe in S.

Use the field automorphism
$`\sigma(\sqrt2)=-\sqrt2`$, $`\sigma(i)=i`$. Its literal native
identities are

```math
\sigma(T_P^{\pm1})=P T_P^{\pm1},\qquad
\sigma(K)=\epsilon_K K,\qquad \epsilon_K\in\{1,-1\}
```

for an actual Clifford word K. Also $`\sigma(F)=F`$, since the
controlled Pauli has entries in $`\mathbb Q(i)`$. Booleanity makes
sigma(V) a constant scalar multiple of V. That scalar has modulus one:
sigma sends every displayed native factor to a unitary. Absorb the
literal endpoint signs into this scalar lambda. Applying sigma to the
whole word and canceling actual factors gives

```math
E_B\mathsf P_AE_B^\dagger
 =\lambda\mathsf P_B F\mathsf P_C F.              \qquad(3)
```

Put $`e=\langle p_C,h\rangle`$ and $`D_b=(-1)^{b(z)}`$.
Every product centralizes R, so the right side of (3) is a Pauli
unitary times $`D_b^e`$. If e=1, its Clifford displacement image lies
in R. The actual independent native B image on the left has displacement
image equal to its activated axis span. Those axes would therefore all
lie in R, contradicting their activation by the R-centralizing
$`\mathsf P_A`$. The active set is empty; then (3) would force D_b
to be Pauli, contrary to the genuine quadratic part of b. Hence e=0.
The right side is now Pauli, so independence again makes B activation
empty. Thus

```math
[E_B,\mathsf P_A]=0,\qquad
\mathsf P_A=\lambda\mathsf P_B\mathsf P_C.
```

Since $`\mathsf P_A`$ commutes $`\mathsf P_B`$, their product is
Hermitian. Hermiticity of $`\mathsf P_C`$ forces lambda to be a sign,
giving (2) without a separate classification of global scalar phases.
Both endpoint products now commute H and every middle axis.

To centralize the opposite endpoint, use

```math
V(K_A^\dagger\mathsf P_AK_A)V^\dagger
 =K_C^\dagger E_C^\dagger\mathsf P_AE_CK_C.
```

The [Boolean-reality argument](COMPUTATIONAL_PHASE_DERIVATIVES.md#7-boolean-reality-gives-a-shared-computational-center)
applies in reverse. The left side is a Pauli times a real Boolean
derivative; the right side is one independent native-band image.
Its intrinsic displacement puts every activated C axis in computational
Z coordinates. Squaring the real derivative makes the activated label
sum zero, and native independence makes the active set empty.
Therefore E_C centralizes $`\mathsf P_A`$. The forward argument
applied to $`\mathsf P_C`$ similarly makes E_A centralize it. Equation
(2) then centralizes $`\mathsf P_B`$ through all factors as well.

The paid real-quadratic alignment can make these products computational
simultaneously with the earlier center. They activate neither endpoint
list, and their total products remain fixed for these native lists.
They supply no further iteration of the preceding constructions.
This Galois argument is on the actual full-space logical word; it does
not apply the automorphism to an unspecified unused clean extension.
In particular these shared logical products are not automatically
clean-odd flips for the original physical one-clean circuit.

## 5. A fixed Clifford decoder exists only under centralization

Let E be any independent native signed Pauli T/T-dagger band on the
full space, and let S be an isotropic Pauli-label space. Then

```math
E^\dagger\mathrm{Alg}(S)E\text{ is a Pauli algebra}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{every native axis of E commutes with S}.       \qquad(4)
```

Consequently a fixed Clifford can decode the left-hand algebra to a
Pauli algebra if and only if the right-hand condition already holds.

For the nontrivial direction suppose the image equals Alg(S'). For
every s in S, the exact one-band expansion of
$`E^\dagger P_sE`$ has a nonzero coefficient on its original Pauli
label s. Each activated rotation contributes a nonzero cosine, and
independence makes the activated subset labels distinct, so no other
term cancels that coefficient. It follows that S is contained in S'.
The two algebras have the same dimension, namely
$`2^{\dim S}=2^{\dim S'}`$, so S=S'.

If an axis a activates some s, the same expansion also has a nonzero
coefficient at s+a. Membership in Alg(S') then gives s+a in S, hence
a in S. That contradicts isotropy because a anticommutes with s.
There can be no activated axis. The converse is immediate.

A Clifford conjugation only permutes signed Pauli labels and maps
Pauli algebras to Pauli algebras, so a fixed Clifford decoder cannot
evade (4). Independence on the full space and isotropy of S are both
used. The theorem does not apply to a dependent restricted fiber list.
It also does not exclude the known paid native decoder E, another
non-Clifford decoder, or a direct argument about the completed phase.

## 6. The actual witness retains a nonconstant translation

The [actual no-shared-flip CCZ source](ACTUAL_NATIVE_CENTER_WITNESS.md#4-the-surviving-center-and-encoded-algebra)
has retained endpoint band and control

```math
E=T_{X_t}T_{X_tZ_rZ_s}^\dagger,\qquad
F=Z_t^{rs},\qquad R=\mathrm{span}\{Z_r,Z_s\}.
```

Its proof home supplies the original one-clean three-native-band word,
all independent physical lists, the literal unused extension and actual
inverse. In the retained logical form the two native axes are
independent. When r+s=0, E is I; when r+s=1,
$`E=\omega^{-1}S_{X_t}`$. Therefore

```math
E^\dagger Z_tE=
\begin{cases}
Z_t,&r+s=0,\\
Y_t,&r+s=1.
\end{cases}
```

The work translation is $`y\mapsto y+r+s`$. Its displacement center,
its nonzero characteristic sum and its endpoint total-axis product are
all the already computational direction Z_rZ_s. Thus the combined
constructions above retain this nonconstant translation. The middle
list is empty. On the active control sector rs=1, r+s=0, so the exact
occupied word is the degree-three sign CCZ.

The encoded algebra generated by the center and this Clifford image
has dimension eight and twelve Pauli support labels. A dimension-eight
Pauli algebra has eight support labels, and Clifford conjugation
preserves that count. This independently illustrates (4) on an actual
source. It does not assert that the target itself needs a non-Clifford
decoder: its displayed native implementation already exists.

The remaining task is a global constraint on the completed central
phase, retaining the literal native encoders and their phase relations.
On a center fiber even a fixed total-axis parity does not make a native
band Clifford: eliminating one variable from four positive T phases
leaves the exponent

```math
2(x_1+x_2+x_3)-2(x_1x_2+x_1x_3+x_2x_3)
 +4x_1x_2x_3\pmod8.
```

This contains genuine non-Clifford terms. Declaring the fiber encoder
Clifford, choosing a conditional decoder without its native synthesis,
or deleting the formal quartic target part would leave an operation
unpaid. These mechanism limits do not settle the unrestricted
three-band degree question and do not change the complete resource
frontier.
