# Clifford rigidity on a non-Pauli clean code

[Current frontier](../../docs/OPEN_PROBLEM.md) ·
[Three-layer native structure](THREE_LAYER_NATIVE_STRUCTURE.md) ·
[One-band matching](ONE_BAND_AXIS_MATCHING.md)

This proof home controls the ambiguity between two **actual full-space
Cliffords** that agree on a non-Pauli clean code. It then applies that
rigidity to native probes of a two-band Pauli image. The latter image
is not assumed Clifford merely because its occupied action agrees with
one. The result is a structural component for the open one-clean
three-layer degree question, not a degree theorem or a change to the
complete-frame resource frontier.

## 1. The exact pointwise Clifford stabilizer

Let E be a native T/T-dagger band on independent commuting signed
Hermitian Pauli axes. Let P be a signed Hermitian Pauli and put

```math
S=EPE^\dagger,\qquad \Pi=(I+S)/2.
```

Suppose exactly k band axes anticommute with P, with $`k\ge1`$, so S
is non-Pauli. Write $`L_S`$ for their binary span and p for P's label.
Then S is a traceless Hermitian Clifford, its symplectic displacement
has image $`L_S`$ and rank k, and its Pauli support is the full coset
$`p+L_S`$, with coefficient magnitudes $`2^{-k/2}`$. Physical axis
independence prevents collisions in this native expansion.

**Pointwise stabilizer theorem.** A Clifford C satisfies the literal
identity $`C\Pi=\Pi`$ precisely as follows:

- If $`k\ne2`$, then $`C=I`$ or $`C=S`$.
- If $`k=2`$, the possibilities form an exact Klein four group. In
  fixed Clifford coordinates on two qubits v,t, they are

```math
\begin{aligned}
W_0&=|0\rangle\langle0|_v\otimes Y_t
       +|1\rangle\langle1|_v\otimes I_t,\\
W_1&=|0\rangle\langle0|_v\otimes I_t
       +|1\rangle\langle1|_v\otimes X_t,\\
\{C\}&=\{I,W_0,W_1,S=W_0W_1\}.
\end{aligned}
```

All spectators are acted on identically. Here $`W_0`$ is a
negative-control Y and $`W_1`$ is CNOT. Their phases are fixed by
$`C\Pi=\Pi`$; the conclusion is not merely projective.

### 1.1. The support budget for k at least three

A Clifford with symplectic displacement rank r has Pauli support on
one affine coset of that displacement image, with $`2^r`$ coefficients
of magnitude $`2^{-r/2}`$. A nonzero identity coefficient makes the
coset linear. The retained [flatness proof](NATIVE_BAND_SOURCE_STRUCTURE.md#3-native-right-extension)
uses trace orthogonality on the kernel of the symplectic displacement;
the [coset argument](CONTROLLED_PAULI_TRANSPORT.md#the-exact-c3-tableau-test)
uses its fixed-label constraints and support translations. Both apply
with the actual common scalar retained.

Pointwise fixation is equivalent to

```math
C+CS=I+S.
```

With normalized full-space trace, put $`a=\mathrm{tr}(C)`$ and
$`b=\mathrm{tr}(CS)`$. Then $`a+b=1`$. If either trace vanishes,
the other is one, and unitarity forces $`C=S`$ or $`C=I`$. A nonzero
rank-zero trace similarly makes the corresponding operator scalar and
gives those same alternatives. Every additional solution therefore
has displacement ranks $`r,s\ge1`$, with

```math
|a|=2^{-r/2},\qquad |b|=2^{-s/2}.
```

Ordering $`r\le s`$, the triangle inequality permits only
$`(r,s)=(1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(2,2)`$. Both supports contain identity.
For $`k\ge3`$, the support of $`I+S`$ has at least nine positions.
Only $`(r,s)=(1,3)`$ and $`k=3`$ can reach nine; the two supports
must then intersect only at identity. The rank-one Clifford contributes
a nonidentity coefficient of magnitude $`1/\sqrt2`$, whereas every
nonidentity coefficient of $`I+S`$ has magnitude $`1/\sqrt8`$.
This is impossible, proving the theorem for $`k\ge3`$.

### 1.2. The one- and two-axis cases with literal phases

The Paulis commuting S have exactly the binary space

```math
G=L_S^\perp\cap p^\perp,\qquad G^\perp=L_S+\langle p\rangle.
```

Indeed the independent transvection sum for S has kernel
$`L_S^\perp`$; there its commutation signs are those of P. Every
Clifford fixing $`\Pi`$ pointwise fixes each such Pauli literally.
For if $`CQC^\dagger`$ and Q differed but agreed on the code, their
quotient could not be an anticommuting Pauli product, whose eigenvalues
are $`\pm i`$. A different commuting product would be a nontrivial
signed Pauli fixing the entire half-dimensional code. Equality of
dimensions would then make S Pauli, a contradiction.

Thus C's Pauli support lies in $`G^\perp`$. This space has symplectic
rank two and radical dimension $`k-1`$: its radical is generated by
the sums of one active axis with each other active axis. Clifford
coordinates reduce its algebra to one quantum qubit with $`k-1`$
commuting Z controls and identity on spectators. All conditional
one-qubit blocks of a Clifford have the same binary symplectic action:
conjugating the core X and Z gives fixed core Pauli labels, with only
control-dependent signs.

For $`k=1`$, signed axes and orientations put S in the literal form
$`(X+Y)/\sqrt2`$ on the core. The one-qubit Clifford action on Bloch
vectors is an orientation-preserving signed permutation. Only identity
and the half-turn about this bisector fix its oriented vector. Fixing
the eigenvector's phase selects exactly I and S.

For $`k=2`$, choose active axes $`Z_t,Z_tZ_v`$ and $`P=X_t`$.
Signs and T-dagger orientations are absorbed by a control-bit flip and,
if necessary, conjugation by $`X_t`$; an overall P sign is changed by
$`Z_t`$. The resulting literal blocks are $`S_0=Y_t,S_1=X_t`$.
A pointwise-fixing Clifford has the same core symplectic action on both
blocks. It must fix both the X and Y axis labels, so that action is
identity and both blocks are Paulis up to scalar. Fixing each occupied
eigenvector leaves precisely $`C_0\in\{I,Y\}`$ and
$`C_1\in\{I,X\}`$. All four choices are the actual Cliffords
displayed in the theorem. This finishes the proof.

## 2. A uniform literal comparison gap for Clifford restrictions

Let S now be any traceless Hermitian native Clifford and
$`\Pi=(I+S)/2`$. Let A,B be native Clifford representatives with their
literal phases. Then

```math
(A-B)\Pi=0\quad\text{or}\quad
\|(A-B)\Pi\|\ge1-1/\sqrt2.
```

No separate code-preservation hypothesis is needed here. Put
$`W=B^\dagger A`$, $`a=\mathrm{tr}(W)`$ and
$`b=\mathrm{tr}(WS)`$. The normalized squared Hilbert--Schmidt
distance on the code is

```math
\|(W-I)\Pi\|_{2,\mathrm{code}}^2
=2-2\mathrm{Re}(a+b)
\le\|(W-I)\Pi\|^2.
```

A nonzero native Clifford trace of displacement rank r is
$`2^{-r/2}`$ times an eighth root. Flatness gives its magnitude;
multiplying the trace by $`2^{r/2}`$ stays in
$`\mathbb Z[i,1/\sqrt2]`$ and has modulus one, so the retained
[unit-circle lemma](CLEAN_AWARE_DEPTH_OBSTRUCTION.md#42-exact-dirty-return-and-the-explicit-zero-clean-floor)
gives its phase. If W or WS is scalar, the other trace is zero, and
the assertion follows directly. Otherwise each positive real part of
a or b lies in $`\{2^{-j/2}:j\ge1\}`$. Their sum is at most one.
Its largest possible value strictly below one is
$`1/\sqrt2+1/4`$: the next value $`1/\sqrt2+1/(2\sqrt2)`$
exceeds one, while $`1/2+1/2=1`$. Zero or negative real parts only
decrease it. Hence nonzero distance is at least

```math
\sqrt{2-2(1/\sqrt2+1/4)}=1-1/\sqrt2.
```

This is a literal-phase lower bound, not an optimality claim. It does
not apply to a general two-band Pauli image merely because its code
restriction is close to a Clifford, and it supplies no robust Boolean
degree theorem by itself.

## 3. Native probes of a compressed logical image

Return to the one-band code S of Section 1. Suppose

```math
A=E_BJE_B^\dagger,\qquad A\Pi=C\Pi,\qquad
[A,S]=[C,S]=0,
```

where J and C are Hermitian Cliffords and $`E_B`$ is an actual native
band with independent axes spanning L. The one-clean three-layer split
supplies these objects for every preserved logical Z image. It also
supplies $`S=E_BJ_cE_B^\dagger`$ with $`J_c`$ Clifford. Define

```math
W_S=L_S+\langle p\rangle,\qquad
N=L^\perp\cap W_S^\perp.
```

For each Hermitian Pauli R with label in N,
$`ARA=E_B(JRJ)E_B^\dagger`$ is Clifford, while CRC is Pauli.
Both preserve the code and agree on it. If $`k\ne2`$, Section 1 gives
the exact identities

```math
ARA=(CRC)S^{\nu(R)},\qquad \nu:N\longrightarrow\mathbb F_2.
```

The character nu is linear: multiply two conjugation identities and
retain the identical Pauli multiplication phase on both sides. S
commutes every CRC. The argument also covers noncommuting probes.

If nu is nonzero, one such ARA has the same symplectic displacement
as S. Native independence makes that displacement image the span of
the $`E_B`$ axes active on JRJ. Therefore

```math
\nu\ne0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L_S\subseteq L.
```

Put $`W=CA`$. Then $`WRW^\dagger=RS^{\nu(R)}`$, so W
centralizes every Pauli in $`\ker\nu`$. If h represents nu on N
under symplectic duality, Pauli expansion gives

```math
\begin{array}{ll}
\nu=0: & \mathrm{supp}_P(W)\subseteq K=L+W_S,\\
\nu\ne0: & \mathrm{supp}_P(W)\subseteq
                 K=L+\langle p,h\rangle,
                 \quad\mathrm{rank}_{\mathrm{symp}}K\le4.
\end{array}
```

Thus the nonzero-character branch has at most two quantum coordinates,
with possibly many commuting controls. For $`k=2`$, the same argument
uses a homomorphism from N to the exact Klein four group. Its members
centralize all Paulis commuting S by Section 1.2, so probe products
again respect that homomorphism. Its kernel has codimension at most
two. One may take $`K=L+W_S+\langle h_1,h_2\rangle`$, whose
symplectic rank is at most ten, giving at most five quantum coordinates.
The nonzero-character displacement argument is not asserted for this
exceptional group.

### 3.1. The support statement retains the actual native factors

More generally, suppose $`W=CE_BJE_B^\dagger`$ is supported on K
and $`L\subseteq K`$. Every Pauli R in $`K^\perp`$ commutes W
and $`E_B`$, hence

```math
E_B(JRJ)E_B^\dagger=CRC.
```

A one-band image is Pauli only if the input Pauli commutes every native
axis: otherwise independent axes give distinct noncancelling expansion
terms. Thus $`JRJ=CRC`$. It follows that CJ centralizes
$`K^\perp`$. Moreover CRC commutes every B axis, so each conjugated
axis $`CbC`$ commutes R; varying R gives $`C(L)\subseteq K`$.
Consequently the literal factorization

```math
W=(CE_BC^\dagger)(CJ)E_B^\dagger
```

has both independent native bands and its Clifford middle supported
in the same Pauli space K. The scalar phases and actual inverse band
remain in this identity. A symplectic rank $`2r`$ algebra acts on r
quantum coordinates with commuting controls and identity spectators.
This is a structural description of an existing circuit, not a free
compiled coordinate change or an uncharged supplied source.

## 4. A common logical defect space and its remaining width dependence

In the zero-character branch the space $`K=L+L_S+\langle p\rangle`$
can have unbounded quantum rank. The shared clean identity
$`S=E_BJ_cE_B^\dagger`$, with both sides Clifford, does permit
[native axis matching](ONE_BAND_AXIS_MATCHING.md): $`J_c`$ permutes
the B axes, and S acts on those axes identically because $`E_B`$
commutes them. Since S is a one-band Pauli image, its symplectic action
is $`I+N_S`$ with $`N_S^2=0`$. Independence of the B list forces
every orbit to be a singleton or a pair. If there are m pairs, their
differences are independent and are precisely a basis for $`N_S(L)`$.
Independence of the clean-active axes identifies the pairing map with
this displacement map, giving

```math
\mathrm{rank}\langle L,L_S\rangle=m,\qquad
\mathrm{rank}_{\mathrm{symp}}(L+L_S)=2m,\qquad
\mathrm{rank}_{\mathrm{symp}}K\le2m+2.
```

In the nonzero-character branch $`L_S\subseteq L`$ forces $`m=0`$.
Therefore, for $`k\ne2`$ and $`m>0`$, every member of the complete
logical family $`W_z=C_zA_z`$ has zero character and is supported on
the **same** $`K=L+W_S`$. Section 3.1 gives $`C_z(L)\subseteq K`$.
Since $`C_z`$ commutes S, it preserves the full support coset
$`p+L_S`$, hence $`W_S`$. Thus $`C_z(K)=K`$ for every z.
This is a joint invariant space, rather than separate coordinates for
different generators, but its rank may grow with m.

The clean-matching pair phases can be reoriented, with their fixed
Clifford corrections retained, to commute with the clean image in the
rearranged split. This
does not show that they commute with every occupied logical Z. Peeling
them as diagonal logical factors would be unsupported. A small number
of quantum coordinates also does not by itself bound the Boolean
complexity of the commuting controls.

## 5. Arbitrarily large rank still need not supply a shared clean flip

This family extends the two-wire example in
[three-layer structure, Section 9](THREE_LAYER_NATIVE_STRUCTURE.md#9-what-fails-when-one-clean-qubit-is-allowed).
It also shows that the pair count m above is a property of the displayed
native split, not a lower-bound invariant of the target.

Use pairs $`(c_j,d_j)`$ for $`j=1,\ldots,m`$, with **only**
$`c_1`$ initialized. All other $`2m-1`$ wires are arbitrary data.
In operator order, with the rightmost gate acting first, define

```math
\begin{aligned}
K_j&=\mathrm{CX}_{c_j,d_j}H_{c_j},&
C_j&=\mathrm{CX}_{c_j,d_j}H_{c_j}\mathrm{CX}_{c_j,d_j},\\
E_j&=T_{c_j}T_{d_j},&
B_j&=C_j(T_{c_j}T_{d_j}^\dagger)C_j^\dagger,\\
L_0&=\prod_{j=2}^m\mathrm{CX}_{c_j,c_1},&
K&=(\bigotimes_jK_j)L_0.
\end{aligned}
```

The gates in $`L_0`$ commute and $`L_0=L_0^\dagger`$. In
particular $`L_0Z_{c_1}L_0^\dagger=\prod_jZ_{c_j}`$. Put
$`E=\bigotimes_jE_j`$, $`B=\bigotimes_jB_j`$ and use the actual
encoders $`U_A=EK`$, $`U_B=B^\dagger EK`$. Their common clean image is

```math
S=\prod_jJ_j,\qquad
J_j=\frac{(X_{c_j}+Y_{c_j})(X_{d_j}+Y_{d_j})}{2}.
```

Each $`B_j`$ commutes $`J_j`$ literally. S has intrinsic active rank
$`2m`$ and its +1 sector is one half of the full space. There are not
m initialized conditions.

A physical Pauli H having a Pauli preimage under $`U_A`$ must be
Z-type: every other H expands through the independent Z axes of E.
It flips S exactly when its total Z weight is odd. The literal native
convention $`T_P=(I+P)/2+\omega(I-P)/2`$, $`\omega=e^{i\pi/4}`$,
gives $`B_j=\omega^{-1}T_{XX}T_{YY}`$. Direct conjugation yields

```math
\begin{aligned}
E_j^\dagger B_j(ZI)B_j^\dagger E_j
   &=(ZI+IZ+XY-YX)/2,\\
E_j^\dagger B_j(IZ)B_j^\dagger E_j
   &=(ZI+IZ-XY+YX)/2.
\end{aligned}
```

The simultaneous E rotation fixes $`XY-YX`$, so all four terms
survive. I and ZZ remain Pauli. Tensor products cannot cancel the
distinct terms in an odd-weight pair. Hence common Pauli preimages
are exactly the Z-type Paulis having even weight in **every** pair;
all commute S. No shared Pauli clean flip exists for this split, even
for $`m\ge2`$, where Section 1 makes the pointwise Clifford stabilizer
only $`\{I,S\}`$.

The complete transition has the fair simplification

```math
\begin{aligned}
V&=U_B^\dagger U_A=K^\dagger E^\dagger BEK
  =L_0^\dagger(\bigotimes_jV_j)L_0,\\
V_j&=\mathrm{CX}_{c_j,d_j}(T_{c_j}T_{d_j}^\dagger)
                         \mathrm{CX}_{c_j,d_j}.
\end{aligned}
```

Indeed $`[ZI+IZ,XX+YY]=0`$, so the literal E and B bands commute.
Also $`C_j=K_j\mathrm{CX}_{c_j,d_j}`$, which gives the displayed
$`V_j=K_j^\dagger B_jK_j`$ with its native scalar intact.

| Full word | T count | T-depth | Elementary H/S/CNOT |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Displayed split | $`6m`$ | $`3`$ | $`12m-2`$ |
| Actual inverse of displayed split | $`6m`$ | $`3`$ | $`12m-2`$ |
| Simplified baseline | $`2m`$ | $`1`$ | $`4m-2`$ |
| Actual inverse of baseline | $`2m`$ | $`1`$ | $`4m-2`$ |

Only H and CNOT occur in the Clifford words, so their actual inverse
costs are unchanged; each T orientation is reversed. All identities
hold on the full physical space. The target is diagonal, returns the
sole clean qubit, and has zero leakage and zero error at every precision.
The arbitrary data may be reference-entangled. They are not being
claimed as returned identity dirty helpers: the target acts on them.
No other work is allocated.

S swaps the two B axes in every pair, so the clean pair count is m.
Nevertheless V is full-space diagonal, and all logical-Z encoder
comparisons agree on the full space: every $`W_z=I`$. Thus unbounded
active rank and unbounded pair count coexist with a one-layer target.
This diagnoses a failed inference for the displayed representation;
it is neither a hard-query family nor a resource improvement.

The [exact fixtures](../../tests/test_clean_code_clifford_rigidity.py)
check the low-rank pointwise ambiguity and the native counterexamples,
including literal phases and complete inverse actions. They support
the sign and ledger conventions; the arguments above establish the
width-independent component theorems.

## 6. The precise remaining extension problem

The pointwise theorem applies when both compared operations are actual
Cliffords. In a general one-clean three-layer split,
$`A_z=E_BJ_zE_B^\dagger`$ is a two-band Pauli image. The equality
$`A_z\Pi=C_z\Pi`$ does not establish that $`A_z`$ or
$`C_zA_z`$ is Clifford on the unused sector. The following actual
correlated native split shows that this inference is false.

### 6.1. A non-Clifford image with a Clifford occupied restriction

Use clean c and arbitrary data t. Put

```math
\begin{aligned}
D&=(S_tH_t)\mathrm{CX}_{t,c},&
E_0&=D(T_c^\dagger T_t)D^\dagger
     =T_{Y_t}T_{Z_cY_t}^\dagger,\\
E&=T_cT_t,&
U_A&=EH_c,\qquad U_B=EH_cE_0.
\end{aligned}
```

The two axes of $`E_0`$ are independent physical Pauli axes. Its
clean-zero block is I, while its clean-one block is
$`(I-iY_t)/\sqrt2`$. Both encoders therefore have the same clean
image $`S=(X_c+Y_c)/\sqrt2`$, with $`\Pi=(I+S)/2`$. Their
logical Z images are exactly

```math
C=U_AZ_tU_A^\dagger=Z_t,\qquad
A=U_BZ_tU_B^\dagger
 =\Pi Z_t+(I-\Pi)\frac{X_t+Y_t}{\sqrt2}.
```

Thus $`A\Pi=C\Pi`$, both images commute S, and the full clean image
is shared literally. But $`AZ_tA=SZ_t`$ is not Pauli, so A is not
Clifford. This also realizes the nonzero probe character of Section 3:
$`N=\langle Z_t\rangle`$ and $`\nu(Z_t)=1`$.

The complete transition is $`U_B^\dagger U_A=E_0^\dagger`$.
Its displayed one-band/two-band split has six T gates, at most three
layers and ten elementary Cliffords; cancelling the actual adjacent
inverse words gives a two-T, one-layer, eight-Clifford baseline. Each
actual inverse has the same respective costs: $`D(T_cT_t^\dagger)D^\dagger`$
and $`D(T_c^\dagger T_t)D^\dagger`$ each use two CNOT, two H and
four S after expanding S-dagger. Only c is initialized. The occupied
transition is the literal identity on arbitrary t, with exact return,
zero leakage and zero error, including references. The unused block
is retained and the actual inverse is charged. This is a failure of an
extension inference, not a difficult target. The one-layer baseline
preserves the specified full unused-sector extension. For the occupied
identity target alone, the empty circuit is already an exact realization.

### 6.2. Resumption question

The probe theorem supplies a common native support space and isolates
the remaining paired-axis contribution. The useful resumption question
is whether the **complete logical family** constrains those pairs
enough to extract a logical factor of uniformly bounded Boolean degree,
after handling the unused-sector action or constructing an alternative
paid native representation, independently of their number. The blanket
full-space Clifford inference is false even for correlated native
encoders; Section 2's restriction gap cannot repair it. Section 5 rules
out treating the pair count itself as hardness. Conversely, an exact
one-clean three-layer family of unbounded
Boolean degree would settle this test in the other direction, but must
retain every literal phase, address operation, actual inverse, and
clean/dirty return.

For Boolean targets, the retained initialized identity-isolation theorem
already reduces sufficiently accurate approximation to this exact
question, as Section 6.3 records. No additional approximation-to-exact
extension of the two-band logical images is required.

The component results are exact operator statements. They charge no
free source or coordinate circuit and make no new synthesis ledger
claim beyond the explicit witnesses in Sections 5 and 6.1. The
unrestricted one-clean three-layer degree
bound, unrestricted one-clean four-layer rigidity, and dimension-growing
depth lower bounds remain open. R96's general upper law, R79's selected
Result D and R97's scoped obstruction are unchanged.

### 6.3. Boolean approximation reduces to the exact occupied action

The following is a consequence of the retained
[R74 initialized identity-isolation theorem](FOUR_LAYER_CROSSING_RANK.md#51-quantitative-rigidity-on-the-initialized-sector),
not a new degree bound. Put

```math
\varepsilon_*=2^{-530690}.
```

Suppose a native circuit V of T-depth at most three, with at most one
initialized clean qubit and arbitrary dirty width, approximates a Boolean
diagonal sign $`G=G_{\mathrm{data}}\otimes I_D`$ on the entire
initialized input space with operator error strictly below
$`\varepsilon_*`$. Exact clean return is not an
assumption. Then V has exactly the occupied action G. If the error
instead permits a freely optimized common phase, the conclusion is
$`V_0=\gamma G`$ for one eighth root gamma. These statements include
arbitrary external references and full dirty return.

To prove this, pad the three-layer word to four slots with one empty
middle band. Its middle crossing rank is zero. For one clean qubit,
R74 gives

```math
\mathcal T_1(0)=38\cdot27931=1061378,\qquad
2^{-[\mathcal T_1(0)+2]/2}=\varepsilon_*.
```

Let $`\Pi_0`$ be the input clean-zero projector. Apply that theorem
first with P=I and then with each computational Z on every logical and
dirty wire. Since G preserves all these Paulis, it forces the same
actual circuit to preserve $`\Pi_0`$ and every $`\Pi_0Z_i`$ exactly.
The threshold is simultaneous; no union bound is needed. An idle clean
wire covers the zero-clean case. Thus the occupied block V_0 is a
diagonal unitary, with exact clean return.

Every diagonal entry of V_0 belongs to
$`\mathbb Z[i,1/\sqrt2]`$ and has modulus one, so the retained
[unit-circle lemma](CLEAN_AWARE_DEPTH_OBSTRUCTION.md#42-exact-dirty-return-and-the-explicit-zero-clean-floor)
makes it an eighth root. In the literal metric, a nontrivial ratio to
G has distance at least $`2\sin(\pi/8)`$ from one. With a free common
phase, two distinct ratios have minimum possible maximum distance
$`2\sin(\pi/16)`$ from that phase. Both exceed
$`\varepsilon_*`$, proving the respective exact conclusions.

Hadamard conjugation on a Boolean XOR query's output preserves T-depth
and turns it into a Boolean sign, so the same reduction applies to
literal returned queries. Consequently a uniform exact one-clean
three-layer degree obstruction would immediately have this explicit
width-independent approximation floor. The exact degree obstruction
itself remains open.

The later [occupied logical transport proof](OCCUPIED_LOGICAL_TRANSPORT.md)
obtains full-space identities from native pair differences instead of
extending occupied Cliffords. It gives a paid common diagonal pair-center
normal form and a stronger counterexample: every logical generator can
remain non-Clifford off-code at arbitrarily high clean rank, even with
no pairs and no shared clean flip. The target is still occupied identity.
