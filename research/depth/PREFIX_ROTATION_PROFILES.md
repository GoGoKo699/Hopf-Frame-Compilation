# Prefix-selected arbitrary rotation profiles

Fix $`L\ge6`$, put $`N=2^n`$, and choose $`0\le q\lt n`$. The top $`q`$ levels of the prescribed real Hopf frame may be arbitrary. At each lower depth and each value $`a`$ of the initial $`q`$-bit prefix, choose one real plane rotation. Its angle is shared by the lower nodes of that depth with prefix $`a`$. In particular the positive magnitude convention allows arbitrary real-valued magnitudes on its permitted interval, with no arithmetic grid. The proof also permits signed rotations if the entire chosen rotation is constant within the stated class. All references to prefix are to computational wires at the time the lower layers act. Preparation from the distinguished input alone would leave the marker columns undetermined.

At square-root dirty width the complete native depth is
```math
 O_L\!\left(\log(q+\log(n+4)+2)\right)
```
whenever $`\exp((q+\log(n+4))^{C})=o(\sqrt{2^n}/n)`$ for the fixed construction exponent $`C`$, with fixed-$`L`$ constants allowed. In particular the conclusion holds for any $`q=n^{o(1)}`$, with $`o(\log n)`$ depth, and it is doubly logarithmic for any $`q=(\log n)^{O(1)}`$. The one-clean input, $`O_L(\sqrt N)`$ T gates, $`O_L(N)`$ elementary Cliffords, arbitrary dirty inputs, actual inverses and full-operator precision include all three pieces below. On the wide branch the direct count and width are bounded by $`\exp(O_L((q+\log(n+4))^C))`$, with an appropriately chosen $`C`$. At smaller widths $`b\lt \sqrt N/n`$ within R96's allowed range use R96, where $`N/b^2`$ covers the logarithmic term. For the stated asymptotic families this proves the usual all-admissible-width simultaneous tradeoff with $`N/b^2`$ added to the displayed depth. For $`q=0`$, use the [homogeneous rotation bank](DIRTY_ROTATION_BANK.md) directly.

## 1. The top prefix

Let $`Z`$ denote all $`n-q`$ lower logical bits for this step. The product of the first $`q`$ layers is the full $`q`$-qubit prefix frame if $`Z=0`$, and the identity otherwise. This follows since at each such layer all lower bits must vanish for the rotation to act. Compute $`H=[Z=0]`$ into the external clean bit to fixed operator tolerance with a complete returned XOR. This is a fixed-depth Boolean AND, covered by the [three-basis Boolean-phase construction](POSITIVE_HAAR_FINGERPRINT.md#dense-succinct-sign-tables) and the Hadamard transform on the XOR target. Its native depth is $`O_L(\log\log(n+4))`$, and both its count and private dirty width are $`\exp(\mathrm{polylog} n)`$. In the hybrid proof first take this check to be ideal.

Compile the prefix frame $`U`$ at fixed tolerance by R96, with $`O_L(q+2^{q/2})`$ available dirty qubits, its one clean wire $`c`$ taken from $`Z`$, and any further dirty wires separate. Condition the entire emitted native unitary on $`H`$. On $`H=1`$, $`c`$ is known to be zero. On $`H=0`$ this physical controlled unitary is identity on every input including an occupied $`c`$. The actual reverse of the predicate restores $`H`$ on the ideal trajectory, and the error of both predicate calls is paid by telescoping. No second external clean qubit is supplied. The controlled circuit depth is $`O(D_U+1)=O_L(\log(q+2))`$, by the coherent controlled Clifford+T construction (Theorem 6 in the cited paper). If needed, pad the compiled register to four wires with returned dirty wires. The T count is $`O(T_U+\text{width})`$. The elementary Clifford normal forms there have polynomial gate count in the physical width, in addition to the retained circuit Cliffords and polynomial cost per controlled piece. This is $`2^{O_L(q+1)}`$. Literal phase is required on the active branch: use the phase of the actual emitted word. In the controlled-interlayer construction its product of Cliffords is corrected on the inactive branch by the actual inverse. If a canonical Clifford representative drops an eighth-root scalar, restore that known scalar by the corresponding power of $`T`$ on the control (negative control by $`X`$ conjugation). It can be identified by finite classical evaluation of the native Clifford word and its representative. The correction is constant size. [Theorem 6 and its explicit construction](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24982v1#S4.SS2) · [R96](ALL_PRECISION_LINEAR_DEPTH.md)

## 2. A single bank selected by the prefix

For the lower block keep the original $`q`$-bit prefix as control. Reserve $`r=O_L(\log(n+4))`$ original bottom bits for the R80 last-one tag and twirl seed. If fewer sites remain, go straight to the terminal below. Use the protected homogeneous stage on the other sites as in the [dirty rotation bank](DIRTY_ROTATION_BANK.md). Its angle bank must now implement
```math
 \sum_a |a\rangle\langle a|\otimes \bigotimes_j R_y(\theta_{a,j})
```
on the initialized seed subspace. To construct it, supply each physical target site $`j`$ with a distinct cohort of dirty candidate registers for each $`a`$. In cohort $`(a,j)`$ use exactly the reflections for the desired angle from the single-profile proof. Apply the tensor of these reflections across *all* cohorts in one call. The selection swap at site $`j`$ uses the first initialized candidate in the cohort named by the actual prefix, and is identity if none exists there.

With ideal selection, on a fixed prefix the other cohorts commute with the swap. Their two adjacent occurrences in the conjugation cancel exactly, including their off-code action. The single-site normalized trace and subsequent Pauli-twirl bounds therefore depend on the width of **one** cohort. The native words for the individual reflections never touch the prefix. A common short coherent seed works for every specified list: in the finite twirl estimate use a union over $`n2^q`$ site/cohort pairs. This adds only $`q+\log n`$ to the logarithm in the tail bound. Since $`q\le n`$ and one-cohort width and inverse requested error are polynomial in $`n`$ at fixed $`L`$, the seed still uses $`O_L(\log(n+4))`$ clean-on-activity bits. Pauli control tables can be applied on all cohorts. Their masks on spectators cancel between the mask and its actual inverse. Each exact Pauli-mask row reads the short seed and an affine parity, with all elementary Clifford costs charged.

For joint approximate routing, add the prefix equality $`[x_{1:q}=a]`$ to each first-good-candidate test in the three XOR sweeps. The sign of each full Fourier-transformed sweep has a fixed-depth Boolean circuit over AND, OR and parity, of size $`\mathrm{poly}_L(n)2^{O(q)}`$ on at most that many bits. The [Boolean-phase construction](POSITIVE_HAAR_FINGERPRINT.md#dense-succinct-sign-tables) gives a full-error, zero-clean implementation in depth $`O_L(\log(q+\log(n+4)+2))`$ and count and private width $`\exp(O_L((q+\log(n+4))^C))`$. Its explicit bound follows by taking polynomial degree $`O((\log S+\log P)^D)`$ for a $`D`$-deep Boolean circuit on $`P`$ variables of size $`S`$, then parallelizing its monomials. Its error is paid a constant number of times for the entire routing word, irrespective of the number of profiles. Prefix preservation is exact in the ideal routers and ideal sector argument. A full operator hybrid pays any leakage from replacing them with approximate routers. The activity and last-one-tag controls remain outside the bank. The same selective-$`Z`$ echo as in the homogeneous construction therefore cancels on inactive sectors even with occupied seed bits.

## 3. The terminal

After the protected stage, the remaining rotations on the bottom $`r`$ sites depend on the first $`q`$ bits only. They are exactly the legal complete frame on those $`q+r`$ sites with all top $`q`$ rotation angles zero and the specified rotations below, tensored with identity on the intervening sites. Apply R96 once after the saved activity bit has returned, with its ordinary one external clean and an allocation of $`O_L(q+r+2^{(q+r)/2})`$ dirty wires. Its depth is $`O_L(\log(q+r+2))`$, and its counts fit the wide budget. Omit the preceding top prefix if $`q=0`$; if $`n-q\le r`$, this terminal, with the appropriate top angles for the entire frame, suffices alone. Use fixed fractions of $`2^{-L}`$ for the prefix, joint routing and terminal. The number of whole-operator substitutions is constant.

The prefix promise permits $`2^q(n-q)`$ independent lower-angle choices in addition to the arbitrary top frame. In particular $`q=\lfloor(\log_2 n)^2\rfloor`$ (capped at $`n`$ in small dimensions) still gives doubly logarithmic depth. The worst-case frame permits angle data depending on the entire running prefix, for which this argument supplies no general improvement.

## Sparse changes to the Haar frame

The [bounded-nesting theorem](SPARSE_HAAR_DEFECTS.md) allows polynomially many arbitrarily located rotations with a bounded number of changes on each branch. Its proof uses a local Haar basis for each antichain of changed nodes. The [sparse word lookup](SPARSE_WORD_AND_FRAME.md) gives a separate square-root dependence of T-count and work on support size when only a bounded number of levels change.

The tests in [test_prefix_rotation_profiles.py](../../tests/test_prefix_rotation_profiles.py) check finite complete matrices and dirty-work identities. The asymptotic statements above follow from the constructions and resource estimates.
