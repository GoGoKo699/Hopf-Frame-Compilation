# Exact dirty copying as a parity grading

[Encoded carrier and copy contract](ENCODED_DIRTY_COPY_INTERFACE.md) ·
[Field-shear query](FIELD_SHEAR_INTERTWINER.md) ·
[Returned cubic compiler](DIRTY_CUBIC_PHASE_COMPILER.md)

A fixed interaction can copy the labels of a noncommuting operator
family. Its exact algebraic criterion is the vanishing of every word
trace with nonzero occurrence parity. This gives necessary tests,
including constant operator-error floors from spectral multiplicity.
It also separates two facts about the field shear: its orbit pairs
cannot approximate anticommuting reflections, but a copyable orbit
can still be genuinely noncommuting.

The criterion supplies an abstract copy interaction, not a native
compiler for its coherent sign intertwiners. The explicit example below
has a Clifford seed and copy in wire-permutation coordinates; its
direct query retains linear depth in the address length. Moving it to
the inexpensive field query leaves the seed and copy unpaid. No new
general frame-depth bound follows.

## 1. The exact full-space criterion

Let $`R_1,\ldots,R_m`$ be unitary operators on a d-dimensional
carrier A. They need not commute or be involutions. On an arbitrary
m-qubit physical output Y, the required fixed unitary C satisfies

```math
C(R_i\otimes I_Y)C^\dagger=R_i\otimes X_{Y_i}
\quad(1\le i\le m).                                  \qquad(1)
```

Every carrier and output input, including every external reference, is
included. For a word w in the generators and their inverses, let
$`R_w`$ be its ordered product and $`\pi(w)\in\mathbb F_2^m`$
its occurrence-parity vector. An inverse contributes the same parity
as the corresponding generator. Write $`\tau(A)=d^{-1}\mathrm{tr}(A)`$.

**Exact criterion.** Such a C exists if and only if

```math
\tau(R_w)=0\qquad\text{whenever }\pi(w)\ne0.           \qquad(2)
```

For necessity, multiply (1) in the order of w and take the trace.
Conjugation preserves the trace, while a nonidentity output X word
has trace zero. Independent identity helpers multiply both traces by
their dimension. They cannot repair a failure, even if C mixes them.

For sufficiency, let $`\mathcal A`$ be the unital operator algebra
generated by the R's and their adjoints, and let $`\mathcal A_v`$
span the words of parity v. If $`v\ne u`$, (2) gives
$`\tau(B^\dagger A)=0`$ for $`A\in\mathcal A_v,B\in\mathcal A_u`$.
These spaces therefore form an orthogonal direct sum with
$`\mathcal A_v\mathcal A_u\subseteq\mathcal A_{v+u}`$ and
$`\mathcal A_v^\dagger=\mathcal A_v`$. For a mask s, multiplying
component $`\mathcal A_v`$ by $`(-1)^{s\cdot v}`$ is consequently
a well-defined, trace-preserving star automorphism $`\alpha_s`$.

It has a unitary implementer on the original carrier. Indeed a
finite-dimensional operator algebra is a direct sum of matrix blocks
$`M_{n_j}\otimes I_{r_j}`$. An automorphism permutes isomorphic
blocks and is inner within them. Trace preservation gives equal ranks
for the permuted central identity projections, hence equal
multiplicities $`r_j`$. The block permutation and inner unitaries
therefore extend to a unitary $`P_s`$ on A with

```math
P_sR_iP_s^\dagger=(-1)^{s_i}R_i.                     \qquad(3)
```

Choose one implementer per s. In the joint X basis of Y, define

```math
C=\sum_sP_s\otimes|s_X\rangle\langle s_X|,
\qquad X_{Y_i}|s_X\rangle=(-1)^{s_i}|s_X\rangle.     \qquad(4)
```

This unitary satisfies (1) literally. The implementers need not obey
a group law. Equation (4) does not price coherent control on arbitrary s.

### Dimension and the commuting special case

Each parity component contains a unitary word $`U_v`$ and obeys
$`\mathcal A_v=U_v\mathcal A_0`$. Thus

```math
\dim\mathcal A=2^m\dim\mathcal A_0\le d^2,
\qquad d\ge2^{m/2}.                                  \qquad(5)
```

If the R's are commuting Hermitian involutions, expansion of their
joint sign projectors using (2) gives rank $`d/2^m`$ for every
one of the $`2^m`$ patterns. They form an independent virtual bank
with equal multiplicities. The
[invariant-stabilizer diagonal-seed exclusion](FIELD_SHEAR_INTERTWINER.md#10-a-diagonal-seed-in-an-invariant-stabilizer-basis-cannot-suffice)
therefore still applies to that ansatz even for a noncanonical C.
A genuinely noncommuting family need not have joint sign projectors.

## 2. Robust word tests and spectral multiplicity

For actual unitaries C and R, suppose the maximum error in (1) is eta.
Telescoping a word of length ell, treating inverses literally, gives

```math
\left\|C(R_w\otimes I)C^\dagger
      -R_w\otimes X^{\pi(w)}\right\|\le\ell\eta.
```

For nonzero parity, normalized traces prove

```math
\eta\ge\frac{|\tau(R_w)|}{\ell}.                    \qquad(6)
```

There is a stronger test when $`R_w=e^{i\phi}J`$ for a Hermitian
involution J of nonzero trace:

```math
\eta\ge\frac2\ell.                                 \qquad(7)
```

The Hermitian involution $`C(J\otimes I)C^\dagger`$ has unequal
positive and negative eigenvalue multiplicities. In contrast,
$`J\otimes X^{\pi(w)}`$ has equal multiplicities. Their operator
distance is exactly two: the larger positive or negative eigenspace
of one intersects the opposite eigenspace of the other. The common
scalar phase changes no norm. Thus a tiny normalized trace does not
remove the constant floor in (7).

For example, let $`R_1=Z\otimes I`$ and $`R_2=Z\otimes J_0`$,
where $`J_0`$ is a Hermitian involution of nonzero trace. Both
generators have trace zero, but their parity-nonzero product is
$`I\otimes J_0`$. Every copy has error at least one, even when
the normalized trace of $`J_0`$ is arbitrarily small. These complete
operator tests survive arbitrary identity helpers; initialized-sector
comparisons do not justify them.

## 3. Unbalanced field queries force a common commuting sector

Let Q be a Hermitian involution with nonzero trace, let R be any
Hermitian involution, and put $`S=QRQ`$. Then

```math
\|RS+SR\|=2.                                        \qquad(8)
```

Choose the larger eigenspace $`H_\sigma`$ of Q, for
$`\sigma\in\{1,-1\}`$. The space
$`K=H_\sigma\cap R H_\sigma`$ has dimension at least
$`|\mathrm{tr}(Q)|`$ and is R-invariant. On K,
$`Q\psi=\sigma\psi`$ and $`QR\psi=\sigma R\psi`$,
so $`S\psi=R\psi`$. The anticommutator is $`2I`$ on K and
has norm at most two everywhere. This proves (8), and supplies a
common eigenvector on which R and S have the same sign.

Every nonzero field-shear query, including identity helpers, has
positive trace. Its group law exchanges $`R_a`$ and $`R_b`$
through $`Q_{a+b}`$. Equation (8) therefore holds for every pair
in any Hermitian seed orbit. If covariance is only approximate,
$`\|S-QRQ\|\le\epsilon`$, the triangle inequality instead gives

```math
\|RS+SR\|\ge2-2\epsilon.                            \qquad(9)
```

This operator statement stays constant even when the imbalance is an
exponentially small fraction of the dimension. It does not assert
commutation everywhere.

### A sharp pairwise decoding gap

If a fixed unitary decoder maps R and S within maximum error delta
of anticommuting Hermitian involutions A and B, then

```math
\delta\ge\gamma=\sqrt{2-\sqrt2}.                    \qquad(10)
```

Transport their common same-sign eigenvector through the decoder.
For $`\delta\lt\sqrt2`$, the two signed target expectations are
each at least $`1-\delta^2/2`$. Anticommutation makes the sum of
their squares at most one: every real combination $`aA+bB`$ has
norm $`\sqrt{a^2+b^2}`$. Hence $`2(1-\delta^2/2)^2\le1`$,
which proves (10); larger delta already meets the bound. It is sharp
for this pairwise problem: $`R=S=Z`$ and targets
$`(Z+X)/\sqrt2,(Z-X)/\sqrt2`$. Sharpness for a complete family
is not asserted.

Hermitian involutions with scalar commutation relations can only
commute or anticommute. Thus (8) forces a field orbit of that type to
commute. A genuinely noncommuting copyable field orbit also needs
$`\mathcal A_0\ne\mathbb C I`$ in (5), since its commutators have
parity zero. Nonscalar commutators are not excluded.

## 4. A noncommuting all-size example and its native limitation

For $`p\ge2`$, put $`m=2^p`$ and label m carrier wires by
$`\mathbb F_2^p`$. Choose independent nonzero a,b and set

```math
D_x=\exp(i\pi Z_xZ_{x+a}Z_{x+b}/8),
\qquad R_x=D_xX_xD_x^\dagger.                         \qquad(11)
```

Each Hermitian involution flips precisely bit x with a phase.
Every word with nonzero occurrence parity therefore moves every
computational column, proving (2) for all words, in every order.
Ordinary copy $`C=\prod_x\mathrm{CX}(A_x;Y_x)`$ satisfies (1):
it commutes with every diagonal $`D_x`$ and copies the X factor.
Wire translations obey $`W_yR_xW_y^\dagger=R_{x+y}`$.
Nevertheless,

```math
R_0R_a=(Z_bZ_{a+b})R_aR_0.                            \qquad(12)
```

The ratio on a computational column is plus one if the bits at b
and a+b agree, and minus one otherwise. This is a nonscalar Pauli,
not an ignored global phase. It lies in the degree-zero algebra.

The fixed seed and copy are native Clifford words in these coordinates.
For $`K=\mathrm{CX}(a;0)\mathrm{CX}(b;0)`$,

```math
R_0=K\frac{X_0-Y_0}{\sqrt2}K^\dagger,
\qquad
\frac{X-Y}{\sqrt2}=(HSH)H(HSH)^\dagger.               \qquad(13)
```

The middle chronological word is H, inverse-S, H, S, H. Expanding
inverse-S as three S gates gives seven Clifford gates; the K walls
add four CNOTs. Thus the fixed seed has eleven Clifford gates, no T
gates and no helpers. Reversing and reducing adjacent S powers modulo
four gives the same bound for its actual inverse. C and its reversed
word each cost m CNOTs and zero T depth. Every carrier/output input
is arbitrary.

The coherent wire-translation query is not free. The direct construction
uses p sequential controlled matching permutations. Each controlled
swap is two CNOTs around one Toffoli, which becomes one cubic phase
between two target Hadamards. The returned eight-band cubic compiler
with one private mask per matching pair gives the conservative bounds

```math
T(W)\le4mp,\qquad D_T(W)\le8p,\qquad
G_{\rm all}(W)\le10mp,\qquad h\le m/2.               \qquad(14)
```

The masks are arbitrary, return after each matching, and may be reused.
The actual reversed query has the same ledger. This linear-in-p depth
does not meet the discovery target; the example is a complete algebraic
calibration, not a depth improvement.

Conjugating this family and its copy on the carrier by an exact
field-shear intertwiner $`E^\dagger`$ gives a noncommuting all-size
field orbit as well. Thus positive field character together with (2)
does not force commutativity. But the native seed $`E^\dagger R_0E`$
and encoded copy remain unpaid. The eight-band field query and the
inexpensive copy above belong to different coordinates and cannot be
combined while omitting that cost.

## 5. Exact fixtures and the remaining construction

`tests/test_dirty_copy_grading.py` checks a Clifford copy for the
anticommuting pair X,Z, the literal native four-wire family (11) and
its actual inverse, and every carrier/output column of its copy.
It also checks the nonscalar commutator, translation covariance, an
unbalanced field query's common commuting sector, the sharp pairwise
constant, and spectral imbalance despite small normalized word trace.
These fixtures support the identities; the all-word and all-size
conclusions follow from the proofs above.

A new candidate must supply its parity grading and native coherent
copy together. A nonzero off-parity trace retires exact copying;
bounded-word tests (6)–(7) can also retire approximate candidates.
For the field shear, replace Pauli-like anticommuting proposals by
an explicit nonscalar-commutator mechanism. Abstract sign implementers
do not pay their coherent synthesis, and the general frame-depth
frontier remains unchanged.
